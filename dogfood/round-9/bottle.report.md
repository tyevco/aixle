## 1. The finished bottle

A 1 × 1.18 × 1 unit flacon on a walnut coaster with a shallow recess: a rounded-box glass body (0.62 × 0.72 × 0.42, `round=0.16`) hollowed by a smaller rounded box, a glass neck bored 0.05 for the cap, a bronze collar sunk 0.015 into the shoulder, a brass cap (rounded cylinder, 0.90..1.18) with a 0.03 dip-stem down the bore that stops at 0.70, above the cavity's top at 0.675; a pink-amber liquid (`material("#d8582c", transmit=0.55)`) filling the cavity to y = 0.575; an ivory label plate on the +z face with raised brass "AIX" (`text` size 0.08, weight 0.02, extruded 0.03). Six asserts all pass: `inside(liquid, body) == 1`, `inside(liquid, cavity) > 0.85` (0.9353), `void(cavity, cap)`, `overlap(collar, glass) > 0.001` (0.002348), `pieces(bottle) == 1`, `clearance(letters, collar) > 0.2` (0.3841). Two lights (warm key azimuth -35, elevation 50, size 1.5; cool rim azimuth 140, elevation 15, power 0.9), four cameras (hero 30/18/1.3; cap `focus="cap"`; label `focus="label"`, `dof=0.3`; low -25/-8/1.1), `set camera hero`, `set environment overcast`. It is close to the plan with one substitution: the body was planned as a `loft` of two rounded rectangles (a tapered flacon) and is a rounded box in the end, because the beauty render bands through a loft's field (see friction). Chrome was planned for the cap and abandoned (white blob); the collar is bronze so the two metals read as two parts. Report: watertight yes, pieces 1, stands yes, one cavity (the air above the liquid, 0.0112).

## 3. Friction log

- `assert void(cavity, cap)` (line 45 then). Expected a failure with numbers like the others. Got `assert (line 45) fails: void(cavity, cap): the cap stays out of the cavity`: no depth, no volume, no point. I knew what was wrong only because I had planted it. `void` should report where the intrusion is (a point) and how much (volume or depth), like `clearance` reports its gap.
- `liquid = (cavity & (box(1, 0.5, 1) | move(0, 0.325, 0)))` with `assert inside(liquid, cavity) == 1`. Expected a liquid that exactly fills the cavity to be fine. `check` was happy (pieces 1, watertight yes, no warning) but every beauty shot banded through the glass on the coincident liquid/cavity faces. Worked around with `(cavity | offset(0.01)) & ...`, which then broke `inside(...) == 1` (0.8818). The contact rule ("overlap by a cell") and `inside == 1` cannot both hold for a filled cavity; nothing in the docs says which to give up, nor that the beauty render bands on coincident faces (the docs only mention mesh seams).
- `body = loft(rect(0.62, 0.42, round=0.16), rect(0.52, 0.34, round=0.13), 0.72)` as a glass body. Expected the beauty render to treat a loft like any shape. Got heavy stair-step contour lines in the liquid behind the glass at every angle; the same program with `box(..., round=0.16)` rendered clean. The doc only says a loft is "a bound rather than a distance" under `surface()`. I had to give up the tapered form. A note under `loft` and under materials with `transmit` ("do not put glass on a loft or a blend; the marcher bands") would have saved four renders.
- `cap = ... | paint("chrome")` under `set environment overcast` and `studio`: a featureless white cylinder, not a metal. The docs say metals are what they reflect and to judge them in the beauty render, but the two neutral skies leave chrome nothing to reflect. Nothing says chrome needs `sunset`/`night` or a busy scene. Gave up on chrome.
- `light("rim", azimuth=140, elevation=15, size=1, power=0.9, color="#bcd4ff")`: no visible difference against the same shot with the rim removed. Expected an edge highlight on the glass and cap. There is no way to see where a light is: no light on the sheet, no diagram, no per-light contribution; the report just echoes the numbers. A `lights.png` (the model with each light alone, or a compass) would make the choice verifiable.
- `camera("low", azimuth=-25, elevation=4, zoom=1.0)`: expected a low shot; the tray was still seen from above at 4 and even at 0 (perspective eye at the model's centre height). The docs do not say elevation is about the model's centre or that negative values are allowed; I found out by trying -8.
- `--elevation 0` on the command line with named cameras: changed `beauty.png` and the sheet's perspective (title says ELEVATION 0°) but left `beauty_low.png` and the others alone, while `report.md` still says `(the sheet and beauty.png use "hero")` with hero at elevation 18. The docs say the CLI "still overrides" without saying it overrides only the anonymous view; the report line is wrong after an override.
- `camera("label", ..., dof=0.6)`: `dof` is in the signature with no scale given ("dof=0"). 0.6 blurred the out-of-focus glass into coarse blocks (visibly low sample count). No doc says what 1 means or that the blur is blocky.
- `set environment sunset`: hero shot's backdrop is flat brown from any camera above the horizon. The docs sell sunset as "a warm band at the horizon under a blue sky", true only for a camera at the horizon. A line saying which cameras see the band would help.
- `callouts.png` title "6 OF 8 NAMED STEPS IN VIEW" and the report's Callouts line: neither names the steps left out, and `report.json` has no callouts entry at all. The glass, the most visible surface, is labelled BODY (the uncut primitive) rather than GLASS (the step the user painted and would look for).
- `steps.png`/`report.md`: the loft/box `cavity` is listed "In output: yes" and drawn without a red frame although it only ever exists as a cutter and as a query region; fine, but the docs say a red frame marks a step that "never reached the output", which a cutter arguably did not. Minor.
- `check` prints no line for `light`, `camera`, `set camera`, `set environment`. A typo in a camera name or an environment name would only surface in the render. Expected `check` to list them as the report does.
- `--quick --beauty` still writes every named camera (4 shots, 11 to 16 s per quick pass). Fine, but a `--camera hero` to render one shot while iterating would halve the loop.
- Speed: the full render with four cameras is 40 to 45 s (evaluate 3.4 s, mesh 3.1 s, hierarchy/atlas 4.9 s, beauty 4 to 5 s per shot, the `focus` label shot 7 to 8 s). `--no-export` would have helped but I did not want to lose the report's numbers; the docs do not say the report survives `--no-export`.
- Sandbox: `sed -i 's|...|...|'` fails on lines with `|` pipes (every .aix line), so edits had to go through `python3` heredocs or the Edit tool. Not the tool's fault, but worth telling agents in the skill: "use the Edit tool on .aix files".
- The one-line failure `assert (line 46) fails: inside(liquid, cavity) == 1 is 0.8818 == 1` is good; the doc's advice "compare a measured size with < and >, not ==" applies to `inside` as well and the examples in the guide (`inside(spring, box) == 1`) push the other way.

## 4. What worked

- `check` in 4 s with every step's box, the assert lines with both sides printed (`0.002444 > 0.001`), and `asserts: N pass, M fail` with a non-zero exit. The passing lines with numbers told me how much margin each promise had.
- The named cameras: `beauty_<name>.png` per camera with the caption baked in, `focus=` framing a step at its own cell (the label lettering is crisp), and the report's Cameras line mapping each camera to its file.
- `--quick --beauty --out DIR` as a materials/lighting loop: 11 to 16 s for all shots, and `--out` kept trials side by side.
- The slices: the wall thickness, the liquid level, the stem stopping in the bore above the cavity were all readable at a glance, and the box-body slice showed the rounded floor immediately.
- The steps sheet and the callout leaders were accurate wherever they pointed.
- `set environment` and `light()` syntax: right first time from the reference alone, no errors.

## 5. Three changes that would have helped most

1. Make the transmissive/beauty path honest about fields that are not distances: warn at `check` when a `transmit` material sits on a `loft`, a smooth `union(k=)` or a scaled shape ("the beauty render bands on an approximate field"), and say so under `loft` and `material(transmit=)` in the docs; also say that two coincident faces (a liquid filling a cavity) band in the beauty render and that the fix is an `offset` of a cell, with the `inside > 0.9` assert that goes with it.
2. Give `void` (and `inside`) a failure with numbers: the intruding volume and a point, e.g. `void(cavity, cap) is 0: 0.0004 of cap inside the region at (0, 0.64, 0)`, and name the unlabelled steps in `callouts.png`'s title and the report ("not labelled: glass, liquid"), preferring the painted/cut step's name (`glass`) over the primitive it was built from (`body`).
3. Make lights and cameras checkable before the render: `check` lists them; the docs state that a camera's elevation is about the model's centre and may be negative for a low shot, that `--azimuth/--elevation/--zoom` override only the anonymous view, and what `dof` values mean; and a `lights.png` (or a per-light strip in `--quick --beauty`) so an agent can see what a rim light contributes rather than guessing from two identical pictures.
