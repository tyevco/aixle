# Round 9 — owl money box (`dogfood/owl.aix`)

## 1. The finished owl

`dogfood/owl.aix` is an owl figurine 2 × 1.49 × 0.84 units (1.49 tall with the ear tufts; 1.41 to the crown), perched on a walnut branch lying on y = 0 and facing +z. The body is `ellipsoid(0.36, 0.34, 0.34) | shell(0.06)` with a coin slot cut in the back (`slot_cut`, 0.16 × 0.035) and a plug hole underneath filled by a separate rubber `plug`; a `neck` cylinder carries the `head` (ellipsoid + two cone tufts + cone beak) on `joint(head_part, "head", 0, 1, 0)`. Eyes are four decals (two ivory spheres, two black pupils) on the head; a cream chest is a decal on the body. Wings are a skin of the body's own surface (`offset(body_solid, 0.05) - offset(body_solid, -0.012)`, intersected with an oval with `k=0.04`), so they hug the body and sink in by one cell. Three rounded-box tail feathers fan down and back; two feet are a leg tube plus three front toes and a back toe as `tube(0.024, curve(arc(...))) | rotate(z=90)` wrapped round the branch. Ten `assert`s all pass (pieces == 1; cavity void at rest and in `look_left`; slot region void; wing overlap in (0.0001, 0.004); both eye regions overlap the head; head clearance > 0.01 in `look_left` and > 0.005 in `curious`). Poses `look_left` (head [0,60,0]), `look_right`, `curious` (`xform(rotate=[0,35,14])`), animation `scan` 4 s, ease 1, ease_ends 0. Lights: warm key (az -35, el 50, size 1.5) and blue rim (az 150, el 35, power 1.2). Cameras: `portrait` (az 25, el 12, zoom 1.3, `set camera portrait`), `eye` (focus="eye_l", zoom 2, dof 1), `back` (az 180, el 20). Environment: tried `sunset` twice, kept `studio`. It came out as planned; the only departures from the plan are the tufts (added) and the wings rebuilt as a surface skin after the first ellipsoid wings read as discs. Seven renders in total. Report: `out/owl/report.md` — watertight no (22 edges, all at feet/branch, feather/body, plug), pieces 1, stands yes.

## 2. Verification

**Asserts (what `check` printed):**
- `assert void(cavity, owl)` — first run **failed**: `assert (line 75) fails: void(cavity, owl): the money box holds nothing` (and the same in pose look_left). No number, no location. I worked out by hand that the legs' round caps (top y 0.405) reached the cavity region (which at x = 0.15 starts at y 0.39); shortening the legs made it print `assert (line 75) holds: void(cavity, owl): the money box holds nothing` — still no number, `void` is 1/0.
- `assert void(slot_region, owl)` — passes; **deliberately broken** by shortening the cutter to `box(0.16, 0.035, 0.1)` so it stops inside the wall: `assert (line 79) fails: void(slot_region, owl): the coin slot goes through into the cavity`, `asserts: 8 pass, 1 fail`, exit 1. Note the failure is printed after all the passes, not in line order, and nothing says where the solid was found. Restored → holds.
- `overlap(wing_l, body) > 0.0001` / `< 0.004`: `is 0.0003787` with the ellipsoid wings, `is 0.001505` with the skin wings; both hold with the numbers.
- `overlap(eye_l, head_ball) > 0.0001 is 0.001345` holds. On a scratch copy with the eye moved off the face (z 0.6) `check` printed both: `warning: line 61: decal(): the region `eye_l` touches none of the shape's surface, so it paints nothing; the region must cross the surface (...)` and `assert (line 82) fails: overlap(eye_l, head_ball) > 0.0001 is 0 > 0.0001`. The decal warning is clear and comes from `check`, not only the render.
- `clearance(head, body) > 0.01, pose=look_left`: `in pose look_left holds: ... is 0.03 > 0.01`; `pose=curious`: `is 0.02488 > 0.005`. Right first time.
- `pieces(owl) == 1 is 1 == 1` every run.

**Poses:** `poses.png` right first time: rest, look_left (head turned to +x, the owl's own left, per the right-hand rule), look_right (far eye visible), curious (turned and tilted). `check --pose curious` gave `posed head_ball x -0.31..0.22`, and `render --quick --pose curious` showed the tilt in front and right views with the title "OWL IN POSE CURIOUS". `anim_scan.png`: 8 frames, bar says `4S LOOP EASE 1 ENDS 0`, the 2.86 s frame shows the back of the head at the look_right key — correct.

**Shots:** `beauty_portrait.png` and `beauty.png` are the same picture (as documented when `set camera` names it). First time (sunset, speckle, ellipsoid wings) it was muddy and the wings stood off the body; after the fixes in studio it is presentable: eyes, beak, chest, filleted wings, blue rim highlight along the right edge. `beauty_eye.png` (focus on the decal region step `eye_l`) worked first time: a full-frame eye with the pupil, beak tip and the other eye at the edge, corners blurred by `dof=1`, caption "CAMERA EYE · EYE_L 0.23 × 0.23 × 0.23". `beauty_back.png` shows the slot as a dark rectangle in the back, the tail feathers below and the wing rims — right first time.

**Environments:** `sunset` (two tries, before and after the material change): a brown owl on a brown-orange gradient with a brown floor; the model barely separates from the background and the rim light is lost. `studio`: grey-white backdrop, clear silhouette. Kept studio.

**Callouts:** "14 OF 14 NAMED STEPS IN VIEW". Every leader points at the step I expect: TUFT (+x copy) and TUFTS (the mirrored -x copy) both labelled; HEAD_BALL, EYE_L, EYE_R, BEAK, NECK, BODY_SOLID (pointing at the body — the shell's outer surface belongs to `body_solid`, not `body`), WING_L, WING_R, PLUG, PLUG_HOLE (a cutter, pointing at the plug), FEET, BRANCH. The decal regions **are labelled as parts** (EYE_L, EYE_R), and so is the cutter PLUG_HOLE. The report's Callouts line lists exactly the same 14 names as the picture, in a different order.

## 3. Friction

- `assert void(cavity, owl), "the money box holds nothing"` (line 75) — expected the failure to say what intrudes and where; got `assert (line 75) fails: void(cavity, owl): the money box holds nothing`, nothing else. I found the legs by arithmetic on the check sizes. The same for the slot failure. `overlap`/`clearance` print numbers; `void` prints only its verdict.
- `cavity = ellipsoid(0.27, 0.25, 0.25) | move(0, body_y, 0)` and `slot_region = box(...)` (lines 19, 21), used only by asserts — every `check` and render prints `warning: 'cavity' (line 19) is not part of the output; add it to the model or remove it`, the sheet's title bar says "2 WARNINGS IN REPORT.MD" permanently, and `steps.png` frames them red "UNUSED". The language doc says a step used only as a decal region is not geometry and is not warned about; an assert-only region is. There is no way to declare a step as a region. This masks real warnings (I stopped reading the count).
- Failing asserts are printed after all the passing ones, not in line order (`line 82, 83` pass then `line 75, 76` fail). Minor, but the eye jumps.
- `callouts.png` labels decal-region steps (`EYE_L`, `EYE_R`) and a cutter (`PLUG_HOLE`) as if they were parts, and `report.md`'s step table says `eye_l ... In output: yes`. Expected regions and cutters to be skipped or marked; nothing in the docs says how a callout picks its name (innermost step whose surface passes there, so the shell is called `BODY_SOLID`, not `body`).
- `set environment sunset` — the doc says "a warm band at the horizon under a blue sky on warm ground"; the actual picture is a brown-to-orange gradient with no visible blue, and a brown model disappears into it. Nothing warns. There is no `--environment` CLI flag, so comparing environments meant editing the file twice (`--quick --beauty` at 18 s each).
- `material("#8a6a48", "speckle", "#5a4128", scale=0.05)` — expected fine feather speckle; the beauty render showed 0.05-unit square blocks (speckle's feature size is a block size, and it is a cubic block pattern). The reference lists pattern names but never shows what each looks like or that speckle is blocky. Switched to `"noise"` at 0.08, which reads as a soft mottle (almost flat).
- `wing_l = ellipsoid(0.09, 0.26, 0.2) | rotate(x=-8) | move(0.43, 0.6, -0.02)` — sunk 0.02 into the body wall exactly as the overlap promise wanted, but a convex ellipsoid against a convex body reads as a disc stuck on. No doc pattern for "a part that hugs a curved surface"; I found `offset(body_solid, 0.05) - offset(body_solid, -0.012)` intersected with an oval, with `intersect(..., k=0.04)` to fillet the rim. Worked, and the overlap assert then measures the one-cell sink cleanly (0.0015).
- `def foot(x) = leg(x) + toe_front(x - 0.045) + toe_front(x) + toe_front(x + 0.045) + toe_back(x)` with `tube(0.024, ...)` — toes 0.045 apart with diameter 0.048 overlap by a hair, and the arc radius 0.115 sank them only 0.009 (under a 0.0125 cell) into the branch: 40 open edges "in 'branch', 'feet'". The report gave positions but not the cause; the language doc's contact rule explains it once you suspect it. Spacing 0.062 and radius 0.108 halved the edges to 22, still at feet/branch (tubes over a tube along an arc) plus `'feather', 'body_solid'` where the feather tips cross the shell at a shallow angle, and 3 in `'plug'`. I could not clear the rest and stopped; renders are unaffected.
- `check --pose look_left`: `posed` lines appear for `head_ball`, `beak`, `tuft`, `face`, `head_part`, but the joint step `head` itself is printed as a swollen box `0.74 × 0.53 × 0.74` with no `surface`/`posed` line. Expected the joint's own line to be posed too.
- Timing: `check` 7 s (pieces + two pose evaluations), `--quick` 14 s (it draws `poses.png` even without asking), the full `--beauty` render 70–81 s of which `poses` 11 s, `anim:scan` 25 s, `hierarchy` 2.5–3.7 s, four beauty passes 4–5 s each; `beauty.png` and `beauty_portrait.png` are the same picture rendered twice. `--no-anims --no-poses --no-export --no-viewer --beauty` at 34 s was the loop that worked for lighting.
- Slices: in the Y = 0.74 slice a black band sits on the ring at the slot (just above the slot's top face); I assume it is the slot's cut faces seen a cell behind the plane, but could not be sure. In the Z = 0 slice the wobbling branch tube draws as a row of overlapping discs (the "outline a cell behind the plane"), which reads as a chain of beads on first sight.
- `arc(r, from, to)` lies on the ground about y; to wrap a toe round an x-axis branch I had to `rotate(z=90)` and work out that θ = 0 lands on +z and θ = 90 on +y. It worked first time, but an `axis=` on `arc` (as `ring` has) or a one-line note would save the derivation.
- The task's "the eyes' decal regions touch the head" promise has no documented assert form; `overlap(eye_l, head_ball) > 0.0001` works (and drops to `0` when the region is off the face), but I guessed it. `inside(eye_l, head_ball) < 1` would be the other half ("crosses, not buried").
- The shell doc pattern is "shell first, then subtract"; with a separate `plug` filling the hole I had to size the plug so it fills the hole's curved inner mouth without entering the cavity margin (plug top 0.335 vs region bottom 0.35), by hand from ellipsoid arithmetic. `void` caught nothing here, but a `surface()`-style query for "the inner surface at this x, z" would have replaced the arithmetic.

## 4. What worked well

- Every assert passing prints its numbers (`overlap ... is 0.001505 < 0.004`), so I could see how much margin each promise had; `pose=look_left` / `pose=curious` on asserts and `check --pose` just worked.
- The decal warning text is exact and actionable, and it fires in `check`.
- `camera(..., focus="eye_l")` framed on a decal-region step, with `dof`, gave a good close-up without any extra `--focus` run; `set camera portrait` steering the sheet's perspective view as well was convenient.
- `poses.png`, the eased `anim_scan.png` bar, and `--pose name --out dir` for a posed full sheet.
- The report's Cameras/Lights/Environment lines, and the "(the sheet and beauty.png use "portrait")" note.
- Slices told the hollow-shell story at a glance (the ring broken at the slot, the plug not entering the cavity).
- The right-hand-rule notes in the docs: `rotate(x=110)` for the beak, `rotate(z=-25)` for the tuft, `head=[0, 60, 0]` for "look left" were all right first time.

## 5. Three changes that would have helped most

1. **Make a failing `void` (and `inside`) say where and what**: the first solid sample it found (position) and the innermost step whose surface is there, e.g. `void(cavity, owl) is 0: solid at (0.15, 0.4, 0.02) in 'feet' (leg)`. Failures should also print in line order.
2. **A way to mark a step as a region/measure-only** (or infer it: a step referenced only by `assert`, `decal` or a query) so it gets no "not part of the output" warning, no red frame in `steps.png`, no callout label and no "In output: yes"; and keep callouts to geometry that reached the output, naming the painted/cut step (`body`) rather than its raw primitive (`body_solid`), and not cutters like `plug_hole`.
3. **Show what patterns and environments look like**: a plate of the ten patterns at their default scale in the reference (speckle = cubic blocks, noise = soft mottle), a thumbnail per environment, a note that `sunset` is a warm brown gradient that swallows brown models, and a `--environment` CLI flag so comparing two takes no file edits. Runner-up: a documented recipe for "a part that hugs a curved body" (`offset(a, t) - offset(a, -cell)` ∩ region with `intersect(k=)`), since the overlap promise pushes you straight into it.
