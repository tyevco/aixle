# Round 9 dogfood: machined mounting bracket

Program: `/home/user/aixle/.claude/worktrees/round9-bracket/dogfood/bracket.aix` (final, 14 asserts passing). Probes: `dogfood/bracket_probe_void.aix`, `_void2`, `_void3`, `_void4` (the `void()` bug, bisected). Renders: `out/bracket/` (final full render with `--beauty`, `--no-export --no-viewer`), `out/bracket_studio/` (quick beauty under `studio`), `out/probe3/` (the void probe's slices). Six renders used of 25; five `check` runs on the bracket, four on probes.

## 1. The finished bracket

A 1.5 × 1 × 1 L-bracket on y = 0: a 1.5 × 0.15 × 1.0 base plate, a 1.0-tall, 0.15-thick upright at the back (−z), a cove fillet in the inside corner (a bar sunk 0.01 into both plates minus a cylinder along x, since a `union(k=)` would also ridge the coplanar back faces), a centre gusset drawn as a side polygon and extruded along x (shortened from 0.82 to 0.5 tall after the quick sheet showed the slot notching it), four bolt holes through the base each as a 0.12 through cylinder plus a 0.22 × 0.06-deep counterbore, a rounded slot 0.5 × 0.12 through the upright, two 0.1 dowel pins in `iron` sunk 0.07 into the base, and "BRK-2041" engraved 0.02 deep into the upright's front face, the body in `steel`. Promises: four `void(rod, bracket)` "goes through" tests on thin rods reaching past both faces, four `void(cbore_*, bracket)`, `void(slot_rod, bracket)`, two `overlap(pin, base) > 0.0004`, `clearance(pin_l, pin_r) > 0.1`, `pieces(bracket) == 1`, `inside(behind, bracket) > 0.999` for a box behind the engraving. Lights: `key` (azimuth −35, elevation 50, size 3, warm) and `fill` (azimuth 70, elevation 25, size 2, power 0.4, cool). Cameras: `iso` (40/32, zoom 1.15, `set camera iso`), `top` (0/90, zoom 1.3), `cbore` (`focus="cbore_fr"`, zoom 2, dof 0.4). Environment: `overcast`, kept over `studio`. It matches the plan in full; the only departure is that the cutters are subtracted one by one instead of as one union, to work around a `void()` bug. Report: watertight no (3 edges in `label`, mesher noise), stands yes, pieces 1, cavities none, overhangs 1.2% (the slot's ceiling and the counterbore lips); there is no overhang query so it cannot be asserted (`overhang(b)` → `unknown function 'overhang' (did you mean overlap?)`).

## 2. Verification

**Asserts, first `check` (unintended failures):**
- `void(cbore_fl..br, bracket)` all failed with no number and no location: `assert (line 56) fails: void(cbore_fl, bracket): front-left counterbore is empty`. The mesh was clean (probe slices at X=0.55, Y=0.12 show an empty stepped hole). Cause, bisected in the four probes: `plate = body - holes` with `holes = hole_fl + ... + cbore_fl + ...`; `void(cb, base - (thru + cb))` is 0 while `void(cb, base - (cb + thru))` is 1 and `void(cb, base - thru - cb)` is 1. Reordering the union made the *through holes* fail instead. Probe 4 narrows it: only a union member that comes after a member it overlaps is misread (three non-overlapping counterbores in one union all pass). `clearance(cb, base - (thru + cb))` agreed with the bug: `-0.05` (exactly cb.r − thru.r), while `inside(cb, h1)` and `overlap(cb, h1)` said 0 (correct). Fix: subtract each cutter on its own; then all pass.
- `overlap(pin_l, base) > 0.001 is 0.0005444 > 0.001` failed: the number is right (π·0.05²·0.07 = 0.00055), my threshold was wrong. Passed at `> 0.0004`.
- `'behind' (line 66) is not part of the output; add it to the model or remove it`: an assert-only region is warned about. It stays warned in the final render (six such warnings, `rod_*`, `slot_rod`, `behind`, all red-framed UNUSED on steps.png).

**Deliberate failures (one check run):** engraving extruded 0.4 (through the 0.15 upright) and slot made 0.1 deep (buried inside the upright). `check` printed `inside(behind, bracket) > 0.999 is 0.6111 > 0.999: the engraving does not break through` and, as a bonus, `pieces(bracket) == 1 is 8 == 1: one piece` (the letter counters became islands). But `void(slot, bracket)` still *held* for the buried slot: a region that is the cutter is empty by construction, so `void(cutter, model)` never proves "goes through"; I switched to independent thinner-longer rods, which is what caught nothing wrong but would.

**Shots.** `beauty.png` = `beauty_iso.png` (as `set camera iso` promises). First time (overcast, silver pins, 0.03 stroke 0.03 deep): presentable but the pins blew out to pure white, the engraving read as black ink, and the pins reflect in the steel upright as ghost pins. `studio` (quick beauty): bluish higher-contrast steel on a darker grey backdrop, pins still white; kept overcast for the even catalogue light and the more legible engraving. Second pass: `iron` pins fixed the blow-out; a 0.04 stroke closed the "0" so it read "BRK-2841" — back to 0.03 (the doc's 3× rule applies to digits too). `beauty_top.png`: elevation 89 and 90 both accepted; the bracket reads well from above (holes, pins, gusset, the engraving foreshortened on the upright), but the backdrop has a visible diagonal horizon seam across the top-left in both environments and both elevations. `beauty_cbore.png`: framed correctly on the front-right counterbore with the pin and the neighbouring hole in frame; the stepped hole reads clearly; but the through hole looks *blind* because the focus frame (0.26 × 0.24 × 0.26) clips the base under it, a ghost "41" (the engraving reflected in the steel) sits on the base beside the hole, `dof=1` blurred everything including the counterbore, `dof=0.4` is right.

**Callouts.** 8 labels: UPRIGHT, BASE, PLATE, GUSSET, FILLET, COVE_BAR, PIN_L, PIN_R; the report's "Callouts (callouts.png, ...)" line lists the same eight, so it matches. No cutter (hole_*, cbore_*, slot, label) was labelled as a part. Not labelled: `body`, `bracket` (the unions), and the cutters. Two things read wrong: `PLATE`'s leader points into the slot (the slot's cut face is credited to the difference step, so the picture says the slot is called "plate"), and `FILLET` and `COVE_BAR` both point at the same cove (a sub-step and the step cut from it label one surface); `UPRIGHT`'s leader also lands on the slot's upper edge.

## 3. Friction

- `plate = body - holes` with `holes = hole_fl + ... + cbore_fl + ...` and `assert void(cbore_fl, bracket)`: expected the counterbores to be empty (the mesh shows them empty); got `assert (line 56) fails: void(cbore_fl, bracket): front-left counterbore is empty` for all four, and `clearance(cb, h1) > -0.001 is -0.05`. `void`/`clearance` misread a later union member that overlaps an earlier one. Worked around by `body - cbore_fl - ... - hole_fl - ...`. This cost most of the session and is the one real tool bug.
- `assert void(cbore_fl, bracket)` failing prints no number and no place: `void(cbore_fl, bracket): front-left counterbore is empty`. Expected at least "solid at (x, y, z)" or a fraction like `inside` gives. I had to bisect with four probe files to find what was in the hole.
- `assert void(slot, bracket)` with `slot = box(0.5, 0.12, 0.1, ...)` buried inside the upright: expected it to fail (the doc says `void` "promises a hole goes through ... with the region the shape you subtracted"); it held. A region that is the cutter is empty by construction. The doc should say to use a probe region longer than the wall, not the cutter.
- `behind = box(...)` used only in `assert inside(behind, bracket)`: `warning: 'behind' (line 66) is not part of the output; add it to the model or remove it`, and six such warnings in the final report, red-framed UNUSED on steps.png. Expected assert-only regions to be exempt as decal regions are. No workaround; lived with six warnings that hide real ones.
- `overlap(pin_l, base) > 0.001 is 0.0005444`: not a tool fault, but the doc's example `> 0.001` invites a threshold in cubic units without saying a 0.05-radius pin 0.07 deep is 0.00055; a hint that overlap is a *volume* and how small volumes are would save a run.
- Overhangs: report says `1.2% of the surface faces down more than 45°`; no query exists to assert it (`overhang(b)` → `unknown function 'overhang' (did you mean overlap?)`). Expected `overhang(shape)` beside `pieces`.
- `camera("cbore", focus="cbore_fr", zoom=2)`: the through hole under the counterbore reads as blind in `beauty_cbore.png` because the focus frame clips the base below y ≈ 0.07; the doc says the sheet draws the clipped rest faint, but the beauty shot just cuts it. Expected the beauty focus shot to keep the whole model (or say it clips). No workaround.
- `camera("top", azimuth=0, elevation=90)`: accepted (89 too), but `beauty_top.png` has a diagonal horizon seam across the backdrop's top-left in both `overcast` and `studio`. Expected a clean backdrop for a top-down shot. No workaround from the program.
- `camera(..., dof=1)`: blurred the pin, the far hole and softened the counterbore itself; the reference says nothing about dof's scale or where the focus plane is for a `focus=` shot. `dof=0.4` worked; found by trial.
- `pin_l = ... | paint("silver")` under `set environment overcast`: pins render pure white (a mirror of a white sky). Expected silver. Worked around with `iron`; the doc's "judge metals in --beauty" could add that overcast turns chrome/silver white.
- `label = extrude(text("BRK-2041", 0.12, weight=0.03), 0.06, "z")`: the engraving renders near-black in every beauty shot (a 0.03-wide, 0.03-deep groove under a size-3 light), reading as ink. Halving the depth to 0.02 helped a little; no doc guidance on engraving depth versus light size.
- `text("BRK-2041", 0.12, weight=0.04)`: the "0" closed and read as "8" on the beauty shot; `check` gave no counter warning (0.04 is ~5 cells). The doc's "size at least three times the weight" is stated for lowercase only; digits need it too.
- Callouts: `PLATE` labelled with its leader inside the slot and `FILLET` + `COVE_BAR` on one surface. Expected a cut face to be credited to the cutter ("slot (cut)") or left unlabelled, and a sub-step of a difference not to get its own label next to the result's.
- Steps sheet draws every cutter (holes, counterbores, slot, label) as a solid part with no mark that it is subtracted; only unused steps get a frame. A "cut" tag would tell an agent a cutter from a part at a glance.
- Language doc "Lights, cameras and environments" and the reference say nothing about: what `zoom` a top-down shot needs (1 left the model in the upper half), whether elevation 90 is allowed, what the environments look like side by side (I had to render both), that `set environment` has no CLI override, and that `set camera` only changes the perspective view's angles (the sheet's front/right/top are unchanged, which is right, but unsaid).
- `union(k=)` for the fillet: the skill recommends it for joins, but two plates sharing a coplanar back face would get a ridge along the seam; the docs never say when a smooth union is wrong. I built the cove by hand.
- `check` on this program takes 6–7 s ("in a second" in the docs) because `pieces()` meshes and fourteen queries sample; a full render at grid 200 with three cameras took 60 s, of which the four beauty passes were 38 s and the export 5 s, and the OBJ is 23 MB / STL 13 MB for a bracket. `--no-export --no-viewer` was the right loop flag and is documented.

## 4. What worked

`check`'s step table caught nothing wrong but confirmed every box in one screen; the passing-assert lines with their numbers (`0.5 > 0.1`, `1 > 0.999`) are exactly what a promise should print; the failing `inside` and `pieces` messages carried the numbers and let me see the letter islands; the `extrude(profile, h, "x")` side-profile convention gave the gusset first time; the quick sheet caught the slot-through-gusset fault at once; `set camera iso` drove the sheet's perspective as promised; the report's Lights/Cameras/Environment/Callouts lines matched the pictures; the misspelt environment warning lists the valid names; `--focus` on a counterbore did frame the right hole; slices and steps were unambiguous.

## 5. Three changes that would have helped most

1. Fix `void()`/`clearance()` on a difference whose cutter is a union with overlapping members (probe files 1–4 are a ready test: `base - (thru + cb)` versus `base - (cb + thru)`), and make a failing `void` print where it found solid.
2. Exempt steps used only as assert regions from "not part of the output" (and from the UNUSED frame), and document in the assert section that `void(cutter, model)` is a tautology: use a probe region past both faces for "goes through".
3. An `overhang(shape)` query (percent of surface down more than 45°) to match the report row, plus a line in the camera docs on what a focus shot clips in the beauty render and on `dof`'s scale.
