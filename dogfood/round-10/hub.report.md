# Round 10: bolted wheel hub flange (`dogfood/hub.aix`)

Files: `/home/user/aixle/.claude/worktrees/round10-hub/dogfood/hub.aix` (the model), `/home/user/aixle/.claude/worktrees/round10-hub/dogfood/hub_probe_onebyone.aix` (the same hub with the cutters subtracted one by one, for the comparison), renders under `out/hub/`, `out/hub_probe_onebyone/`, `out/hub_focus/` and `out/diff_hub_hub_probe_onebyone.png`. Nothing committed; nothing outside `dogfood/` and `out/` touched. 7 renders used (1 quick, 3 full, 1 diff, 1 focus, plus the variant), 5 `check`s, 1 `explain`.

## 1. The finished hub

A steel flange 2 × 0.5 × 2 on y = 0: a disc r 1.0, 0.25 thick with a 0.03 round on the rim; a boss r 0.42 to y 0.5 with a 0.02 round; a bore r 0.2 with a 0.1 × 0.08 keyway at +x; six holes r 0.07 on radius 0.7 made with `ring(6, 0.7)`, each counterbored r 0.12 from y 0.15 to 0.35 (so the counterbore overlaps its hole by 0.1 and is 0.1 deep from the top face); six round pockets r 0.1, 0.12 deep, on the same circle turned half a pitch (`ring(6, 0.7) | rotate(y=30)`); "HB-7" engraved 0.03 into the boss top on an arc of radius 0.27. The cutters are one union subtracted once (`hub = (body - cutters) | paint("steel")`). Nine promises: the six holes go through (`void` on a ring of r 0.03 rods past both faces), the counterbores are empty (`void` on a ring of r 0.1 pucks inside them), the bore is clear, the keyway is open to the bore (`void` on a box spanning bore and keyway), no counterbore reaches the pocket next to it (`clearance(counterbores, pockets) > 0.05`, measured 0.1423), one piece, `overhang(hub) < 0.03` (measured 0.000006), and two about the engraving: the slab under it stays solid (`inside(under_text, hub) == 1`) and the lettering lies on the boss's flat top (`inside(engraved, boss) == 1`). Shots: overcast sky, a size-3 warm key and a size-2 cool fill at power 0.4, `camera("plan", elevation=90)` and `camera("cbore", focus="counterbore_1", elevation=65)`. It matches the plan; the one thing I did not do is a chamfer (I used `round=` on the cylinders, as the docs recommend, "chamfer nothing by hand"). Report: watertight yes, volume 0.792, pieces 1, overhangs 0.0%, no warnings, 9 asserts pass.

## 2. Verification

**Deliberate failures.** With `hole = cylinder(0.07, 0.4) | move(0, 0.25, 0)` (stops 0.05 above the bottom) `check` printed:
`assert (line 38) fails: void(rods, hub), solid at (-0.36, 0.02, -0.59) in 'disc': every bolt hole goes right through` — where and in which step, though the step is the solid (`disc`) rather than which hole. With `pocket = cylinder(0.25, 0.3) ...` (wide enough to break into the counterbores) it printed:
`assert (line 42) fails: clearance(counterbores, pockets) > 0.05 is -0.13 > 0.05: no counterbore reaches the pocket next to it` — a number but **no location**. After the fixes both hold: `void(rods, hub): every bolt hole goes right through` and `clearance(counterbores, pockets) > 0.05 is 0.1423 > 0.05`. The other seven passed from the start with their numbers printed (`pieces ... 1 == 1`, `overhang ... 0.000007682 < 0.03`, `inside ... 1 == 1`). One of my own failed too, usefully: `inside(under_text, hub) == 1 is 0.9935 == 1, under_text is outside hub at (0.25, 0.43, -0.02)` — my probe annulus started at r 0.22 and ran into the keyway (x to 0.25); the point told me exactly that.

**One union versus one by one.** `check` output identical line for line (same 9 asserts, same numbers: clearance 0.1423, overhang 0.000006173); full renders identical: 144184 triangles, volume 0.792, centre of mass (-0.000552, 0.154, 0), watertight, 1 piece, 0.0% overhang; `aixle diff` shows two identical sheets (23128 quick triangles each). **The callouts differ.** With one union subtracted once, `callouts.png` says "2 OF 2 NAMED STEPS IN VIEW" and labels only BOSS and DISC: not one cut face is credited to anything, not even to `cutters`. With one-by-one cuts it says "6 OF 6" and labels BOSS, DISC, BORE (CUT) on the bore wall, KEYWAY (CUT) on the notch, COUNTERBORES (CUT) on the front counterbored hole, POCKETS (CUT) on the pocket right of it. The report's Callouts line says the same (`disc, boss` versus `disc, boss, counterbores (cut), pockets (cut), bore (cut), keyway (cut)`).

**Callouts, read closely (one-by-one version).** The COUNTERBORES and POCKETS leaders each land on a hole of the right kind (a counterbored hole and a flat-bottomed pocket), so no label lands on a hole it does not name. Unlabelled: `holes` (the through-hole walls under the counterbores are never named, in either version), `engraving`, and `hole`/`counterbore`/`pocket` (the single copies, fine). The DISC label's leader ends on the boss's top rim at the back-right, where the disc surface behind the boss and the boss rim meet on screen; it reads as pointing at the boss. In the union version the DISC leader ends at the same spot.

**Steps.** `steps.png` tags `bore`, `keyway`, `hole`, `holes`, `counterbore`, `counterbores`, `pocket`, `pockets`, `engraving`, `cutters` as CUT (drawn as the solid they remove) and `counterbore_1`, `rod`, `rods`, `cb_probe`, `bore_probe`, `key_probe`, `under_text`, `engraved` as REGION (grey frame); no thumbnail is framed red. The report's In output column says `yes / cut / region` for the same steps (and in the variant `bored`, `keyed`, `drilled`, `cbored`, `pocketed` are `yes`). Overhangs row: "0.0% of the surface faces down more than 45° above the floor".

**Slices.** The default y slice sat at y = 0.25, which is the disc's own top face: that panel drew the whole disc face in the outline colour (black) with the boss annulus grey, so filled-versus-surface was ambiguous. `set slice_y 0.2` gave the proper section: twelve holes, bore with its keyway notch at +x. The Z = 0 slice shows both holes at x = ±0.7 as gaps through the full thickness with the wider counterbore on top, and the +x side of the bore wider by the keyway; X = 0 shows a pocket dip on each side and the engraving as a tiny notch in the boss top.

**Focus shot.** `beauty_cbore.png` at elevation 35 (first try): a stepped dark bowl, the hole floor dark with concentric rings, the boss faint behind; it did **not** read as through, because a 0.14-wide hole through 0.25 cannot be seen through below about 61°. At elevation 65 the overcast floor shows through the hole as a lighter tan disc: it reads as through. `beauty_plan.png` reads well: counterbore rings, through holes darker inside, pockets flat, keyway, HB-7 legible. `beauty.png` is good; `lights.png` shows key, fill, all.

**Picture that caught what the asserts did not.** The first full sheet's perspective showed jagged bits on the back rim of the boss: the text arc 0.31 put the letters at r up to 0.425 (the `label` line in `check` said `y 0.26..0.42`, the boss radius, and I did not read it), so the engraving broke out through the rim. My `under_text` promise (the slab under the engraving) could not see that. Fixed with arc 0.27 and a new promise `inside(engraved, boss) == 1`.

## 3. Friction

- `assert clearance(counterbores, pockets) > 0.05` — printed `is -0.13 > 0.05` and nothing else. `void`, `inside` and `overlap` say where; `clearance` does not, and the docs say so only by omission. Because of that I did not notice for one more edit that my pockets sat on the holes (below). With a point I would have seen it at once.
- `pocket = cylinder(0.1, 0.3) | move(0, 0.28, 0) | rotate(y=30)` then `pockets = pocket | ring(6, 0.7)`: I expected pockets at 30°, 90°, ...; `rotate(y=30)` on a shape centred on the y axis does nothing, so the pockets landed on the holes and the clearance came out -0.08 with no location. The fix is `ring(6, 0.7) | rotate(y=30)`. No warning; the language doc's `ring` sentence ("each copy is first pushed out along +x by radius, then turned") is right, but an example of "between the copies" would have saved a round.
- `hub = (body - cutters) | paint("steel")` with `cutters = bore + keyway + holes + counterbores + pockets + engraving`: the agent guide and the skill say a cut face is credited to its cutter as `slot (cut)`. With one union subtracted once **no** cut face is credited at all: `callouts.png` reads "2 OF 2 NAMED STEPS IN VIEW", BOSS and DISC only, and the report's Callouts line likewise. Subtracting one by one gives BORE (CUT), KEYWAY (CUT), COUNTERBORES (CUT), POCKETS (CUT). Either the union of cutters should be walked into its members, or at least `cutters (cut)` should appear, or the docs should say to subtract one by one if you want cut faces named. I would not have known the labels were missing had I not built both.
- `holes` is never credited in the callouts in either version even though six through-hole walls are visible under the counterbores; `engraving` neither. Probably "largest visible" keeps them out, but the sheet does not say which steps it skipped, so "unlabelled" and "not credited" look the same.
- Callouts leader for DISC ends on the boss's top rim (both versions), where the disc behind the boss and the boss rim coincide on screen. I could not tell which surface it meant; a leader that ends on an unambiguous patch of the disc (the front of the flange is wide open) would.
- `camera("cbore", focus="counterbore_1", zoom=1.2, azimuth=35, elevation=35)`: the language doc says "A focus shot marches the whole model, so a hole under a counterbore reads as through". At elevation 35 it did not, and could not: a hole 0.14 across through 0.25 needs the camera above atan(0.25/0.14) ≈ 61°. The doc sentence should carry that caveat (or the camera default of 25 should be flagged for a focus on a hole).
- `npx tsx src/cli.ts render dogfood/hub.aix --focus counterbore_1`: `counterbore_1` is a region (only the camera reads it). The focus sheet drew the **region itself** as a solid tan cylinder 0.28 × 0.24 × 0.28 filling every view, the hub faint behind it; the slices show it as filled; the callouts say "0 OF 0 NAMED STEPS IN VIEW"; the report adds "Close-up watertight: yes at cell 0.00174" for that solid. I expected the frame to be around the counterbore with the real cut in it (as the beauty camera does). A region under `--focus` should be drawn as a frame, not as geometry, or the render should warn.
- Default slice plane y = 0.25 fell exactly on the disc's top face (a 0.25-thick disc on y = 0 is the obvious way to build this). The panel drew the whole disc face in the outline colour with only the boss filled grey, so "inside is filled" was hard to apply. `set slice_y 0.2` fixed it. The tool could nudge a slice plane off a coincident face, as it already does for the sampling lattice, or say "this plane lies on a face".
- `label ... profile 0.39 × 0.16  y 0.26..0.42` in `check`: the number that said the lettering reached the boss rim (r 0.42) was there and I missed it; a picture caught it. A warning "an engraving cutter reaches the edge of the face it is cut into" is probably too specific to ask for, but `text(arc=)` could print the radial span (r 0.255..0.385) as well as the profile box, since with an arc the box is not what you reason about.
- Quick sheet title said `2 × 0.52 × 2` for a model `check` says is 0.5 tall (the full sheet says 0.5). It is the quick cell padding; the skill's "a model 10 tall that should be 1 is wrong" made me stop and worry about 0.02.
- `beauty.png`: the steel boss wall reflects the ring of counterbores as a band of light and dark blobs at its base; at first read it looked like a second row of holes cut into the boss. Nothing wrong, but a metal part reflecting its own cut faces can mislead; worth a line in the "mistakes the renders catch" table.
- `check` takes about 4.9 s on this small model because `pieces` and `overhang` each mesh the model at grid 160; fine, but the doc says "in a second".
- The `void` failure named the step of the solid ("in 'disc'"), which is right, but for a ring of six rods it would help to say which copy (the point gives it, indirectly).
- A full render with `--beauty`, two lights and two cameras is 37 s (beauty 5.4 s, lights 3.8 s, plan 6.5 s, cbore 7.2 s, hierarchy/export 2 s). Acceptable, but a `--no-beauty-cameras` or per-camera flag while iterating on one shot would help; `--camera NAME` exists but I only found it late in the language doc.

## 4. What worked well

- The `void`-on-a-thin-rod pattern for "goes through" and the exact failure text with a point and a step; `inside(...)` failures with the point (found my keyway collision in one read). Passing asserts printing their numbers.
- `(cut)` and `(region)` tags in `check`, the steps sheet and the report's In output column: consistent in all three, and a probe step never warned as unused.
- The plan camera at elevation 90 and the size-3 key under `overcast`: a clean drawing-like shot first time.
- Slices as the truth: the through holes with their counterbore steps and the keyway at +x were all readable at Z = 0 once the y plane was moved.
- `explain` gives the tree with the cutters under `cutters` and the regions listed at the end; `diff` for judging the two versions.
- `ring` for both the holes and the probes; the focus camera framing on a single named copy.

## 5. Three changes that would have helped most

1. Make the callouts credit cut faces through a union of cutters (walk `cutters` down to `counterbores (cut)`, `bore (cut)`...), or at least label them `cutters (cut)`, and say on the sheet which visible steps were left unlabelled; right now the doc's promise holds only for one-by-one subtraction and nothing tells you.
2. Give `clearance` failures a location like `void`/`inside`/`overlap` ("closest at (x, y, z)" or "overlap deepest at ..."): it is the one query the round asked for that says nothing about where.
3. `--focus` on a region should frame it, not draw it as a solid (and its slices/callouts/close-up watertight row should be about the model in that frame, as `camera(focus=)` already does); and the "a hole under a counterbore reads as through" sentence should say the camera has to be steep enough to see the floor through it.
