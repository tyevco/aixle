## 1. The finished ring

A 1.5 × 1.505 × 1 model on y = 0: an ebony plinth, a velvet cushion (`material("#7a1b3d", "noise", "#4d0f27", scale=0.03, rough=0.85)`), a gold torus band (R 0.42, r 0.065) built flat, engraved inside its bottom with "FOREVER" (`extrude(text(...), 0.03, "z") | flip("x") | wrap(R - rr) | rotate(y=180)`, 0.02 deep), a chrome square-section inlay stripe revolved round the outer face 0.015 proud, then stood up with `rotate(x=-90)` and sunk 0.03 into the cushion. The head is a hollow gold bezel cup with two windows cut through front and back and four tube claws (`ring(4, 0) | rotate(y=45)`), holding an 8-sided brilliant made from `prism(8, ...)` intersected with sixteen tilted half-space boxes in a loop (crown 25°, pavilion 40°, table and culet cut by two more), painted `material("#ff3b60", transmit=0.55, rough=0.04)`. Seven asserts all pass: `inside(gem, setting_air) > 0.9` (0.9413), `overlap(gem, bezel)` between 0.0002 and 0.005 (0.001098), `clearance(claws, gem) < 0.01` (−0.0089), `void(outer_probe, inscription)`, `overlap(ring, stand) > 0.0003` (0.000456), `pieces(model) == 1`. Three lights (warm key az −35 el 50, cool rim az 150 el 20 power 0.6, a point "spark" at (0.25, 1.7, 0.3) range 0.8), four cameras (hero az 30 el 10 zoom 1.3, gem focus dof 0.4, gem_soft focus dof 1, low az 60 el −8), `set camera hero`, `set environment sunset`, `set ambient 1.3`. One piece, stands, 45 non-watertight edges (the 1.8-cell lettering and the pavilion/cavity contact, both as `check` predicted). It matches the plan; the only departure is the two windows I added to the cup because the gem read black in a closed one, and the gem needed a lower transmit than I expected.

## 2. Verification

**Asserts.**
- First `check`: `gem` came out `empty` because `gem = stone & table & culet | move(...)` moved only `culet`. `check` said: `warning: line 60: '&' removed everything: the two shapes do not overlap (0.48 × 0.38 × 0.48 at ... and 2 × 2 × 2 at -1, 1.32, -1). '|' binds tighter than '&', so check the parentheses.` and the three gem asserts failed as `inside(...) is 0 > 0.9`, `overlap(gem, bezel) is 0 > 0.0002`, `clearance(claws, gem) < 0.01 is Infinity < 0.01`. Parenthesised; all three then passed with the numbers above.
- `overlap(ring, stand) > 0.0005` failed as `is 0.0003256 > 0.0005, they overlap at (-0.02, 0.4, -0.01)`; sinking the ring 0.03 gave 0.0004558; I set the threshold to 0.0003 from the measurement.
- Deliberate failure: I made the engraving 0.3 deep (`extrude(..., 0.3, "z") | move(0, -0.025, 0.14)`), so it cut clean out through the outer face. **The void assert still held**, because my region was `outer_probe_std` (rotated into the standing frame) while `inscription` is in the flat frame: their boxes never meet, so the promise had been vacuous from the first check, and the only symptom was `overlap(ring, stand)` dropping to 0.0001139 (the band's bottom had been eaten). With the region in the same frame, `check` printed: `assert (line 68) fails: void(outer_probe, inscription), solid at (-0.18, 0.02, -0.5) in 'above': the inscription stays inside the band` and exited 1. The point is right (radius 0.53, on the −z side, past the outer face at 0.485); the step named, `above`, is one of the gem's half-space cubes and has nothing to do with the engraving — it should say `letters` or `inscription`. Restored to 0.03 deep: holds.
- All other asserts passed first time with their numbers printed.

## 3. Friction log

- `gem = stone & table & culet | move(0, band_top + 0.07, 0) | paint(ruby)` — expected the intersection to be moved; `|` bound tighter so only `culet` moved and `gem` was `empty`. The warning quoted above named the cause exactly, so the fix was one pair of parentheses, but the one-screen summary in the skill lists `a & b` without the precedence note.
- `assert void(outer_probe_std, inscription), "..."` — I compared a region in the standing frame against a cutter built in the flat frame; the assert held (`assert (line 69) holds: void(...)`) with the engraving cut clean through the band. No warning that the two boxes never meet. A vacuous promise is worse than none; I only caught it because I broke the engraving on purpose.
- `assert void(outer_probe, inscription)` failing printed `solid at (-0.18, 0.02, -0.5) in 'above'` — `above` is `box(2, 2, 2) | move(0, 1, 0)`, a gem cutter far from that point. The location was right; the step was wrong. Expected `letters` or `inscription`.
- Callouts never labelled the engraving as a cut. Guide says "a cut face is the cutter's, as `slot (cut)`"; for `band_flat = (band_blank - inscription) + stripe` the engraved letters are only ever `band`, on the whole model and on `--focus band`.
- Callouts leader for `stripe` (line `stripe = revolve(rect(0.03, 0.03) | move(R + rr - 0.01, 0)) | paint("chrome")`) ends at the floor near the z-axis label, twice. A thin ring round the band is apparently placed by its box centre, which is empty air.
- Metals: docs say nothing about gold under each sky. Under `studio` (default) gold reads as flat yellow plastic, which is what the first beauty render showed; I had to try all four to learn `sunset`. Cost: four full renders (each re-evaluates 9 s and meshes 9 s even with `--no-export`) because `--environment` takes one name per run.
- Transmit gem: `ruby = material("#d8203c", transmit=0.72, rough=0.04)` then 0.8 read as a dark maroon disc under every sky (it showed the cup's shadowed interior). Docs cover glass shells, liquids and banding, not "a coloured stone in a setting wants transmit around 0.5 and something bright behind it". Got there by trial (three renders).
- `--quick --beauty` is documented as unable to judge glass, so every gem iteration was a full render: 90–120 s with exports, of which `beauty:gem` and `beauty:gem_soft` were 24–25 s each (focus shots march the whole model). Five full renders is most of the wall time.
- `letters` at 1.8 cells: `check` said so up front (`note: Parts between 1.2 and 2 cells thick mesh but often not watertight: 'letters' (1.8 cells)`) and the report's 45 open edges came from exactly there and from the pavilion/cavity contact. Known cost of a 0.012 stroke at a 1.5-unit model; grid 220 was the compromise. Not misleading, just slow.
- `steps.png` draws `below`, `above`, `table`, `culet` (2-unit cubes used only as `&` operands) as full-size parts marked "in output: yes"; they dwarf the model and mean nothing. A `(mask)`/operand tag or hiding them would help.
- `steps.png` gem thumbnail (24) is a dark checker blob; nothing about the stone can be read there. The sheet's checker for transmit is documented but at thumbnail size it hides the shape.
- Wrapped text reading direction inside a band: `letters = extrude(text("FOREVER", ...), 0.03, "z") | move(0, -0.025, 0.005) | flip("x")` then `inscription = letters | wrap(R - rr) | rotate(y=180)`. Docs give wrap's mapping formula and "reads left to right from the front", but nothing about the inside of a ring; I derived the `flip("x")` and could not confirm it in any picture (the lettering is ~30 px even on the focus sheet; the steps thumbnails show `letters` mirrored and `inscription` upright, which is a hint but not a reading from inside).
- `bezel = (cup - cavity - windows) + claws` came out tagged `(region)` because only the asserts read it (the output uses `ring`). Correct by the doc's rule, but surprising for a step that is plainly geometry; I first thought the tag meant it had been dropped.
- `lights.png` thumbnails are small enough that a point light's contribution (`light("spark", position=[0.25, 1.7, 0.3], range=0.8, ...)`) is a few pixels; I could see it but not judge its colour or reach.
- `sunset` at hero elevation 18 gave a brown backdrop the maroon cushion nearly sank into; the docs did warn ("only a camera near the horizon sees the band"), so one extra render, not a doc gap.
- Windows cut into the cup still render as black rectangles (ambient occlusion plus a dark interior); acceptable for a gallery but I expected some light to reach the pavilion through them.
- `overlap(ring, stand)` threshold was my guess (0.0005) against a measured 0.00046 for a torus sunk 0.03; the doc's "take the threshold from the part's size" is right and the pin example helped, but a worked number for a curved part on a flat would have saved one check.

## 4. What worked well

`check` in 7 s with every step's box, the precedence warning naming the line and the fix, and every assert printed with its two sides and the overlap point. The `(cut)`/`(region)` tags in `check` and on `steps.png`. `--camera`, `--environment`, `--out`, `--no-export --no-viewer` composed exactly as documented and ran fine in parallel. `slices.png` and the `--focus band` close-up (own cell 0.00517, its own watertight row blaming `letters`/`inscription`) showed the engraving depth unambiguously. `lights.png` as a concept. `sunset` for the chrome stripe, `elevation=-8` for the low shot and `zoom=0.8` on a focus camera were all right first time from the docs. The loop-built gem from a prism and half-space boxes worked first try once parenthesised. The velvet noise at 0.03 reads as cloth in the beauty render.

## 5. The three changes that would have helped most

1. **Guard the promise queries against frames that never meet, and blame the right step.** When `void`/`inside`/`overlap`/`clearance` get two shapes whose boxes do not intersect, warn "holds trivially: the boxes never meet (a ... at ..., b ... at ...)"; and make a `void` failure name the step whose surface is at the point (`letters`/`inscription`), not an unrelated cube (`above`).
2. **A sky contact sheet and a metals/gems paragraph.** `--environment all` (or an `environments.png` like `lights.png`) so four skies cost one evaluation and one mesh instead of four; and a doc line per metal (gold: flat under studio/overcast, best sunset, olive under night) plus "a coloured stone in a setting reads dark above transmit ~0.6 because it shows the setting behind it; start at 0.5".
3. **Callouts and steps for cuts and masks.** Label an engraving as `inscription (cut)` when its faces are what is visible (it never was, even on `--focus band`); place a thin ring's leader on its surface, not its empty box centre; and tag `&` operands on `steps.png` (or draw them clipped to the result) instead of four 2-unit cubes marked "in output: yes".
