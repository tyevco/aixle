# Round 10, alley: a street corner at night

Program: `/home/user/aixle/.claude/worktrees/round10-alley/dogfood/alley.aix`. Renders: `out/alley/` (final full render), plus `out/alley_size`, `out/alley_low`, `out/alley_sunset`, `out/low_a`, `out/low_b` (experiments) and `out/callouts_crop.png` (a 3x crop of the callouts sheet). Nothing committed; nothing outside `dogfood/` and `out/` touched. 16 renders in all.

## 1. The finished scene

A 6 x 6 x 0.15 concrete slab with its top at y = 0; at the back (-z) a brick shop wall with a recessed, glazed window (a 0.1 glass pane over a warm, faintly glowing interior panel), a walnut door with a brass knob, and a maroon sign board sunk 0.02 into the wall with glowing extruded "CAFE" letters; a lamp post at (2.1, 0, 0.5) built from a column, a cone foot, a collar, and a head that is a shelled glass lantern round a glowing bulb on a short stem under a cone cap, assembled with anchors and `attach` and then moved as one; a `std/furniture` bench (`round(0.02)`-fattened) under the lamp; a litter bin; a `std/plants` tree on the left; two bollards `place`d along the kerb. `scene pavement, shop, lamp, bench, bin, tree, bollards`. Lights: a cool moon (`azimuth=150, elevation=40, power=1.1`), a warm point light in the air just outside the lantern (below and in front of it, `range=3, power=1.6`), and a point light 0.7 in front of the sign (`range=2.5`). Cameras: `wide` (az 30, el 16, zoom 1.15, the `set camera`), `window` (`focus="window"`, az 20, el 8, zoom 0.9), `low` (`focus="bench"`, az -40, el -2, zoom 0.6). `set environment night`, `set ambient 1.6`, `set grid 240`. Promises: `clearance(bench, lamp) > 0.3` (0.589), `void(head_zone, others)`, `overlap(sign, shopfront) > 0.002` (0.02), `pieces(everything) == 1`. It matches the plan; the only compromise is the pavement, which every shot shows covered in wobbly concentric bands from the point lights (friction 2), and a low shot whose lower half is the near pavement at grazing angle, dark.

## 2. Verification

Asserts (`check`, ~4 s per run):
- `overlap(sign, shopfront) > 0.002`: failed on the first check by a real slip (the board's back face exactly on the wall's front): `assert (line 62) fails: overlap(sign, shopfront) > 0.002 is 0 > 0.002: the sign is joined to the shop`. No "where", since nothing overlapped. Fixed by sinking the board 0.02: `holds: ... is 0.02 > 0.002`.
- `void(head_zone, others)`: made to fail deliberately by stretching the region to 6 deep (`head_zone = box(0.6, 0.6, 6) | move(...)`): `assert (line 61) fails: void(head_zone, others), solid at (1.85, 2.86, -2.4) in 'wall': nothing but the lamp head is round the lamp head`. It says where and in which step, as the docs promise. Restored: `holds: void(head_zone, others)`.
- `clearance(bench, lamp) > 0.3`: held from the start, `is 0.609 > 0.3` (0.589 after the bench was fattened).
- `pieces(everything) == 1`: failed as `is 4 == 1`, then `3 == 1`, then `2 == 1`, then `3 == 1` at grid 240, then held. `check` never said which piece; the full render's Pieces row did each time (knob sunk 0.02 into the door; the bulb in the air in its lantern; the lantern set exactly on the collar by `attach`; the bench's top back slat).

Shots:
- `wide`: right first time as a composition. The pavement banding is in every version.
- `window`: first render was a black blob filling the frame (the tree canopy between camera at azimuth -20 and the window); at azimuth 20 it is a good close-up: sign, brick, the glazed window with the lit panel behind it drawing concentric rings centred under the sign light.
- `low`: elevation -6 with no focus put the whole scene small in a lot of sky; with `focus="bench"` it frames the bench, bin, lamp column and door, but at seat height the near pavement fills the lower half as a dark band. Three of my low renders were misjudged because I read `beauty.png` rather than `beauty_low.png` (friction 3).
- `--camera wide`: rewrote `beauty.png`, `beauty_wide.png` and `lights.png`, left the other shots' files untouched. As documented, but the whole pipeline (sheet, steps, turntable, physics) still ran: 30 s at grid 160, 50 s at 240.
- `--environment sunset --camera wide --out out/alley_sunset`: worked without editing the program; the report says `Environment: sunset`; from elevation 16 the backdrop is the warm ground as the docs say, the slab takes a blue cast from the sky, and the dark brick scene stays readable.

Lights (`lights.png`, each alone then all):
- moon alone: rim on the tree crown and the wall's top edge, the shop front in its own shadow, floor near black: correct for a light from behind-right.

So point lights do fall off and are shadowed as documented, but only once outside the glass.

`callouts.png` (15 of 16 named steps): PAVEMENT, SHOPFRONT, TREE, PANE, INTERIOR, DOOR, KNOB, BOARD, LETTERS, LAMP, BENCH, BIN, BOLLARDS point at the right things. `WINDOW_CUT (CUT)` is labelled with a leader to the recess, so a cutter is in the callouts. The regions (`others`, `head_zone`, `everything`) are not labelled. `LANTERN`'s leader ends at a dot at the origin on the pavement in front of the bench, nowhere near the lantern on the lamp head (confirmed on the 3x crop): a step built at the origin and carried into place by its parents is labelled where its own box is. `steps.png`: 35 thumbnails, `window_cut` tagged CUT, `others`/`head_zone`/`everything` tagged REGION and framed grey, no red frame anywhere, none wrong. `report.md` In output column: `yes` for the 31 parts, `cut` for `window_cut`, `region` for the three assert-only steps; `lantern_space` (only the shell's input) is `yes`, which is fair.

## 3. Friction

- `light("lamp", position=[bulb_at[0], bulb_at[1] - 0.14, bulb_at[2]], range=3, ...)` with `lantern = lantern_space | shell(0.05) | paint("glass")`: expected, per language.md ("inside a glass shade is fine, since glass lets its light through dimmed") and the reference, a dimmed lamp; got nothing at all, the LAMP frame of `lights.png` black but for the glows. `position=bulb_at` (inside the bulb) the same. Worked only outside the lantern. Either the doc claim is wrong for a `shell`ed glass box or the transmission is so low it is invisible; no warning said the light was inside anything.
- Every shot shows wobbly concentric bands and blotches on the slab, centred on each point light's foot, and rings on the interior panel behind the window centred under the sign light. Not the material: `paint(material("#9c9a95", rough=0.9))` (plain) showed them as clearly as `concrete` at 0.05 and a `noise` at 0.35. Not the light's size: `size=2` on both point lights (`out/alley_size`) turned the pool into cloud-shaped blotches. Grid 160 and 240 alike. The docs mention contour lines behind glass for lofts and coincident faces, never for a point light on a flat box. I gave up and shipped it; it is the one thing that keeps the wide shot from being presentable.
- `--camera window` and `--camera low` with a `focus=` camera: `beauty.png` and the sheet's perspective took the camera's angles but dropped the focus framing (the caption says `SCENE 6 x 3.53 x 6`), while `beauty_window.png`/`beauty_low.png` in the same folder got the framed shot. The doc line "`--camera NAME` renders one declared shot (the sheet and beauty.png take its view)" made me read `beauty.png`; I spent three renders concluding that `zoom` was erratic (0.6 filled the frame, 0.7 and 0.45 were tiny) before noticing the two files differed.
- `callouts.png` labels `LANTERN` at the origin, where the step's own box is, not on the lamp head where its surface is. `lantern` is built at the origin, unioned into `head`, placed by `head | attach("base", collar, "top")`, `attach` again, then `lamp = (...) | move(lamp_x, -0.06, lamp_z)`. Steps moved by their own pipeline (`bench = f.bench(...) | round(0.02) | move(...)`) are labelled correctly.
- `assert pieces(everything) == 1` failed as `is 4 == 1` (then 3, 2, 3) with no hint which piece. I needed a full render each time for the Pieces row ("then 0.00108 at (0.602, 0.841, 0.213) in 'bench', 'pavement'"). Once the fused row said 2 pieces with the loose one in 'bench' while the Assembly row for the bench object said `Pieces 1`.
- `head_on = head_up | attach("base", collar_up, "top")` and `collar_up = collar | attach("bottom", column, "top")`: attach sets face on face, exactly touching, so the lantern was joined at grid 160 (cell 0.038 bridged it) and a loose piece at 240 (`0.0902 at (2.1, 3.14, 0.5) in 'bulb', 'stem'`). The docs push `attach` as the way to place parts and separately say parts must overlap by a cell, and nothing warns when an attach leaves them touching. Worked around by putting the anchors a cell inside the part (`anchor("base", 0, -0.17, 0)` for a glass bottom at -0.22).
- `bench = f.bench(1.6, 0.45)`: `warning: 'bench' (line 48) has a part ... only 0.03 thick, 0.791 of the 0.0379 cell ... raise the grid (set grid 506)` at every grid I tried; at 240 its top back slat was still a loose piece. `| round(0.02)` fattened the slats to 0.07 and cured the piece, but the warning still reads 0.03 (it reads the library part, not the result), so a warning I cannot silence sits on every check and the sheet's title bar.
- After `round(0.02)` the bench had 67 non-manifold edges "all on the plane y = 0.767"; the report's advice (nudge by a fraction of a cell) worked (`move(0.6, -0.055, 0.5)`), but the nudge is a guess and the second render is the only way to know.
- `note: the lowest point of the surface is at y = -0.15; pipe the model through ground() to rest it on y = 0` on every check and in the report, for a slab whose top is meant to be y = 0. Harmless noise, but noise on a scene the docs themselves describe ("a wide scene on a slab").
- `camera("window", focus="window", azimuth=-20, elevation=8, zoom=0.9)`: the tree stood between the camera and the window and the shot was a black blob with a bit of brick at the edges; nothing said the focus was hidden. 18 s for that shot at grid 160 (three times the other shots), 10 s at 240.
- Full render 55-65 s at this size (grid 240: mesh 5.8 s, exports 5 s, beauty 5.5 s, lights 4.7 s, three shots 22 s, steps 3.5 s); `--camera NAME` still 30-50 s because the sheet, steps, turntable, callouts and physics run too. `--no-export --no-viewer` saved ~10 s. There is no "beauty only" switch for a lighting loop.
- `check`'s `overlap` failure at zero has no "where" (fair) but the doc says "a failure says where the two overlap"; a touching-not-overlapping message ("they touch at z = -2.4") would have named my slip.
- Minor: `--quick` dropped six thin steps (pane, knob, letters, lantern, stem, bench) and said so, so the quick sheet could not judge the pieces I most needed judged.

## 4. What worked

`check` in 4 s with every step's box, the named anchors under each step (which is how I found that `at(lamp, "bulb")` was right), the Lights/Cameras/Environment lines with computed positions, and the asserts printed with their numbers in line order. `position=` accepting a list from `at()` and arithmetic on it. The `void` failure naming the point and the step. `lights.png` as a strip of each light alone, which is exactly how I found the lantern light was doing nothing. `--environment` and `--out` for parallel experiments. The report's Pieces row with volume, centre and step. `steps.png` tagging CUT and REGION and framing regions grey; regions kept out of the callouts. `std/plants.tree` and `std/furniture.bench` dropping in with `move`. The `--quick` sheet reading well enough to place everything in one pass.

## 5. The three changes that would have helped most

1. Make `--camera NAME` write the framed shot to `beauty.png` (or say in the docs and the console that `beauty.png` is the unframed view and `beauty_<name>.png` the framed one), and add a beauty-only mode that skips the sheet, steps and turntable for a lighting loop.
2. Fix or document the point light on a flat surface: the concentric bands and blotches, and the inside-a-glass-shell case that gives nothing (warn when a point light's position is inside any solid, glass included).
3. Make `pieces()` failures name the loose piece as the report does, and make `attach` (or `check`) warn when it leaves two faces exactly touching; with that, label moved-by-parent steps in the callouts where their surface is, not at their own box.
