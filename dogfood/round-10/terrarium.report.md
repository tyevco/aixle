## 1. The finished terrarium

1.6 × 1.99 × 1.6 units, standing on y = 0, front +z. A walnut disc base (r 0.8, 0.24 tall, rounded) with a 0.05-deep recess, a bell-jar dome that is a `revolve` of an exact 2D profile (`rect + circle`), shelled to a 0.05 wall, cut open at the rim and sunk 0.02 into the wood, with a glass knob on top; a speckled pebble band over the recess wall, a rounded soil disc on it (clear of the glass by 0.07), three stones, a `plants.bush(0.25)`, a `plants.grass` tuft, a `plants.tree(0.85)` reaching up into the dome, a navy/tan/red gnome (cone, sphere, cone), and a cream-stemmed mushroom whose cap is `material("#ffc860", glow=1.2)` with a point light just above it; "MOSS & CO" wrapped round the base as an ivory text decal. Eight promises, all passing: `inside(plant|gnome|tree, dome_space) == 1`, `void(wall, innards)`, `overlap(rim_tab, base) > 0.0001`, `clearance(dome, base) < -0.01`, `clearance(soil, dome) > 0.03`, `pieces(terrarium) == 1`. Shot: `camera("hero", azimuth=28, elevation=12, zoom=1.2)`, studio, ambient 1.5, warm key + cool rim + the mushroom point light. The final beauty shows a clean refracting bell over the base with the floor and the wood visible through it, the tree and bush green, the gnome and the yellow cap legible, the label readable; the base reflection under the rim is a dark ring, which reads as a real bell jar's. It came close to the plan; the two departures were the dome (loft abandoned for a revolve) and the join promise (whole-ring `overlap` replaced by a rim chunk plus a negative clearance).

## 3. Friction

- `bell = loft(circle(0.62), circle(0.55), 1.1) | move(...) + (sphere(0.55) | move(...))` then `dome = ((bell | shell(0.05)) - below_rim) | paint("glass")` and `assert clearance(soil, dome) > 0.03`: expected the warning about banding (got it, and it was accurate) but did not expect the *shell* and the *queries* to be wrong. `clearance(soil, dome)` came out `4.163e-17` for a 0.1 gap, and the slices showed the shelled wall curling inward at the rim. The warning only mentions the beauty render; nothing in the docs says `shell`, `clearance` and `overlap` (0.0079 vs 0.0037 true) are unreliable on a loft. Worked around by rebuilding the dome as a revolve.
- `assert overlap(dome, base) > 0.002` with the rim sunk 0.02: `is 0 > 0.002`, and sunk 0.04 and 0.06 both give `0.008201`. The reference says "sampled at 24 cells along the shared box's longest side, so measure parts, not a scene", but the shared box of a rim ring and a base is a 1.24-wide, 0.02-tall slab, so it is a part and still 0. Worked around with `rim_tab = dome & (box(0.2, 0.2, 0.2) | move(0.6, base_top, 0))` and `assert overlap(rim_tab, base) > 0.0001`, plus `assert clearance(dome, base) < -0.01`. The docs' example ("a dome rests on the base and is joined by a little (`overlap`)") is exactly the case it cannot measure.
- `assert void(wall, innards)` failing said `solid at (0.57, 0.49, 0.16) in 'bell'`. Expected the intruding step of `innards` (`gnome`, or `plant`). It names the region's primitive instead, which points a reader at the glass rather than at what poked through it. The position was right; I found the culprit by the coordinates.
- `assert inside(gnome, dome_space) == 1` passed at `0.9068` only failing; with the gnome moved so that its body sat *inside the glass wall* (r 0.50..0.62, wall 0.57..0.62) it still counted 0.9 inside `dome_space`, because `dome_space = bell & above_rim` includes the wall's volume. The docs say "inside wants the solid, not a shell" but do not say that the solid *includes* the wall, so `inside == 1` does not by itself keep a part out of the glass; `void` on the wall is needed too. Worth one sentence in the assert docs.
- `pebbles = cylinder(0.49, ...)` in a `recess = cylinder(0.5, ...)`: the 0.01 radial gap is under the 0.0124 cell, and the report listed 225 open edges in 'recess' and 132 in 'pebbles' with the words "two surfaces pass through one grid cell". The guide's contact rule covers this, but the report named `recess` (a cut) as the step, which sent me looking at the base first; fixed by making the pebble band r 0.53, over the recess wall.
- `light("key", ...)`: the reference says the first `light()` replaces the default key, so to add the mushroom point light I had to redeclare a key and a rim by hand. Expected `light("shroom", position=...)` to add to the default key. A note in the point-light sentence would save a render.
- The glow: `material("#ffc860", glow=1.2)` on the cap reads as a flat yellow disc with no shading at all, in the sheet, the steps thumbnail and the beauty render; it only looks like a lamp once a point light sits above it. The docs say a glow lights only itself, but not that it also loses its own shading at glow above 1.
- Slices X=0 and Z=0 show the bush and the canopy as detached blobs because their stems are off the planes. Not wrong, but a reader checking "is the plant on the soil" gets no answer from `slices.png`; `set slice_x` exists but there is no per-step slice.
- `check` prints every step's box every time (30 lines), and the assert lines are at the end; with `grep` it is fine, but a `--asserts` or `--warnings` mode would make the failure loop faster. Minor.
- `overlap(rim_tab, base)` failure text `they overlap at (0.58, 0.23, -0.09)` is good; `overlap(dome, base)` reading 0 gives no hint that it is a sampling miss rather than a true non-contact. A note when the shared box's shortest side is under a lattice cell would have saved the experiment.
- The beauty caption `TERRARIUM 1.6 × 1.99 × 1.6` at the bottom left overprints the base and the label at a low camera (`elevation=12`); minor, but for a presentation picture it would be better in a margin.
- The task's `set environment studio` vs `--environment overcast` comparison needed a second full render (37 s) into a second `--out`; fine, but a `--environment a,b` pair in one run would halve it for glass, which cannot be judged quick.

## 4. What worked well

- The glass warning fired on the exact line, named the cause list and the fix, and the fix (a revolve) worked first time with no warning and a clean render.
- Every failing assert says where; `inside` gives the fraction and a point outside, `overlap` a point of contact, `void` a point (even if the step name is wrong).
- `(cut)` and `(region)` tags in `check`, on `steps.png` and in the report's "In output" column made it obvious which steps were probes; no "not part of the output" noise for `wall`, `dome_space`, `rim_tab`, `label_region`, `above_rim`.
- `wrap(0.8)` on an extruded `text` as a decal region put the label on the cylinder with no arithmetic, and the decal read cleanly in the beauty shot.
- `std/plants` bush, grass and tree dropped in and looked right; `check`'s per-step boxes made placing them under the dome a matter of reading numbers.
- `lights.png` (each light alone) showed exactly what the point light added.
- `--out` for the overcast comparison kept the main folder intact; `--no-export --no-viewer` shaved time.

## 5. Three changes that would have helped most

1. Make the loft/bound warning (and the language doc's glass paragraph) say that `shell`, `clearance`, `overlap` and `inside` are also unreliable on a bound field, and print the warning at the step that shells or measures it, not only at the `transmit` paint. The wall curling in at the rim and a 4e-17 clearance cost the first two renders.
2. `overlap(a, b)`: sample the shared box at a lattice that resolves its shortest side (or warn "shared box 0.02 thin, under the lattice cell; measure a chunk"), so a dome rim sunk into a base, the doc's own example, can be promised directly.
3. `void(region, shape)` failure should name the step of `shape` whose solid was found (`in 'gnome'`), not the region's primitive (`in 'bell'`); and the `inside` docs should say the un-shelled solid includes the wall's volume, so `inside == 1` needs `void` on the wall beside it to keep a part out of the glass.
