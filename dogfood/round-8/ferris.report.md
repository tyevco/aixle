## 1. The finished scene

A `scene ride, ground, booth, lamp_post`: 6 × 3.79 × 3.4 units on a 0.1-thick slab at y = 0. The ride is two navy A-frames (tubes, a tie, a bearing block each) on charcoal feet, a steel axle along z at y = 2.35, an iron motor housing with three vents on the front bearing, and the wheel: an iron hub, two steel torus rims at z = ±0.285 (radius 1.4), eight spokes per rim, eight iron hanger rods between the rims, a gold glowing beacon on the front rim, and eight gondolas (roof, hollow tub with an oak seat, four posts, a two-arm yoke) alternating red/cream. The wheel is `joint(..., "wheel", 0, 2.35, 0, axis=[0,0,1])`; each gondola is its own `joint("gondola_i", px, py, 0, axis=[0,0,1])` nested inside it, made by `def car(i, name, colour)` called eight times with a named step per call (`gondola_1 = car(1, "gondola_1", "red")`). I chose the def over `place()` because a placed copy has only a yaw about y and cannot carry a joint of its own, and joint names must be unique; a def per copy with a named step is the only form that gives eight independently posable, focusable, assertable gondolas. Poses come from `def spin(name, a) = pose(name, wheel=a, gondola_1=-a, ... gondola_8=-a)`: `quarter` 90, `half` 180, `three_quarter` 270, `full` 360 (the fourth pose so the loop's keys stay under 180° apart). `wheel_turn = angle("wheel")[0]` prints 0/90/180/270 in `check --pose`. Animations: `turn` (rest → quarter → half → three_quarter → full, 8 s, loop, `ease=0, ease_ends=0`) and `start` (rest → quarter, 2 s, `loop=0, ease=1, ease_ends=0`). Eight asserts: floor clearance at rest (both `bottom(gondola_1)` and `at(ride, "gondola_1")[1]`), floor clearance of whichever gondola is lowest in `quarter`/`half`/`three_quarter` via `at(ride, "gondola_7"|"gondola_5"|"gondola_3")[1] > 0.3, pose=...`, `bottom(rims) > 0.6`, `pieces(ride, resolution=200) == 1`, `pieces(booth, resolution=100) == 1`. Final report: 8 pass, no warnings, scene one piece, every object one piece and standing; the ride's mesh has 41 non-manifold edges (a mirrored A-frame face lying on the sample plane z = -0.45, named as such). Ticket booth (striped hut, window cut, oak counter, maroon loft roof, ivory board with raised red "TICKETS") and a lamp post with a glowing head. It came out as planned except for one thing I had to add: a beacon, because eight gondolas 45° apart with alternating colours look identical after every 90° pose.

A strip cannot show a seam hitch. So for the linear loop neither `ease=0` nor `ease_ends=0` did anything (`ease_ends` defaults to `ease`); `ease_ends=0` only mattered for `start`.

**Asserts.** Failures from my bugs (each message was exactly what I needed): `assert (line 98) fails: bottom(gondola_1) > G + 0.2 is -0.6 > 0.3` (the `|` precedence bug left roofs and tubs at the origin), `bottom(rims) > G + 0.5 is -1.44 > 0.6` (I measured the rims before moving them), `pieces(ride, resolution=200) == 1 is 35 == 1` (same precedence bug), `pieces(booth, resolution=100) == 1 is 2 == 1` (roof loft floating 0.1 above the hut; `pieces` does not say which piece). ## 3. Friction log

- `def car(i, colour) = joint(... | anchor("floor_" + i, ...), "gondola_" + i, ...)` → `dogfood/ferris.aix: line 30: '+' does not apply to a string and a number`. The language doc says "`+` joins" strings and there is no `str()` in the reference, so a def cannot name a joint or anchor per copy from its index. Workaround: a third parameter `name` and eight explicit calls. (Also: a `for` loop cannot produce eight differently named steps, and a joint made inside a def "has no name, so no line and no thumbnail", so eight lines it is.)
- Keeping a gondola level in a pose: the doc's relative-angle rule ("a stick at -45 on a boom at 35 lies at -10") was enough to derive `gondola_i = -a`, and `pose()` from a def with parameters (`spin(name, a)`) made it one line per pose. Nothing in the docs says "a child that must stay level counter-rotates by its parent's angle" or shows a pose def with many joints. It worked first time, but a pose def with nine keyword arguments is the price of no loop over joint names.
- Per-pose bounds on a nested joint (`bottom(gondola_7)` in `pose=quarter`): `check --pose quarter` printed gondola_1's own box as `x -0.6..0.025 y 0.73..1.17` (its own -90° turn about its own pivot, lying on its side, with the wheel's +90° not applied) and the `posed` line as `x 1.18..1.62 y 1.75..2.37`. The doc says "anything computed from a joint's shape (its bounds, a ground()) follows the pose", which is true of the step's own joint only. An assert about the lowest gondola in a pose therefore cannot use `bottom(gondola_n)`; it needs `at(ride, "gondola_n")[1]` through the outermost step, which the doc mentions only in the hydraulic-cylinder paragraph. The `anchors` line under `gondola_3` in `check --pose three_quarter` also prints the own-frame point `(1.95, 2.35, 0)` where the world point is `(0, 0.4, 0)`.
- The doc's scenes section says `place()` copies get nodes and `--focus name_3`, but not that a placed copy cannot have its own joint or be posed; I had to infer it from "yaw (degrees about y)". One sentence ("copies that must move on their own are a def called per copy") would have saved the deliberation.
- Line 31 (`... + seat + car_frame | anchor(...) | move(...)` inside the def body): my `move` applied to `car_frame` only; the roofs and tubs of all eight gondolas stayed at the origin. `check` said `warning: line 31: '+ ... | move(...)' applies move to the right side only ... wrap the union in parentheses` and the sizes and the 35-piece assert confirmed it. Excellent warning; still, the multi-line def body made the precedence easy to miss.
- `rims = (rim | move(0,0,-0.28)) + (rim | move(0,0,0.28)) | paint("steel")` painted the second rim only; caught by `warning: 'wheel_body' (line 52) joins painted and unpainted parts`. Same lesson; also caught.
- `assert bottom(rims) > G + 0.5` failed with `-1.44 > 0.6` because `rims` was built at the origin and moved later inside `wheel_body`. My mistake; the message made it obvious in one read.
- `assert pieces(booth, resolution=100) == 1` failed `2 == 1` with no hint which piece; the loft roof was 0.1 above the hut. The full report's "separate pieces" warning names the loose piece; the assert does not. I found it by reading the step boxes (`hut y 0.1..1.3`, `booth_roof y 1.4..1.8`).
- `pieces(ride, resolution=200)` made every `check` 24–35 s and added the same to every render (the quick render took 50 s, 1.1 s of which was meshing). The reference says "costs a moment"; with two `pieces` asserts and four poses it is the whole loop's cost. There is no way to assert on the report's per-object pieces count and no flag to skip asserts on a quick pass.
- Full render 3–4 min: from `Timings`, `poses 23 s`, `anim:turn 56 s`, `anim:start 47 s` (plus 55 s for the temporary third animation), everything else under 10 s. `--no-poses` skips all of it; there is no way to render just one strip or skip strips and keep `poses.png`. A `--focus` render costs the same 3.5 min.
- The quick `poses.png` dropped the rim and spokes (thin at the quick cell) so the wheel was invisible; the quick warning listed `spoke` among six dropped steps, which explained it, but the pose sheet gave no hint of its own.
- My symmetric wheel: five identical thumbnails. Not the tool's fault, but a pose sheet cannot tell me the pose changed; a tiny angle readout per thumbnail (the pose's joint angles in its label) would have.
- `--focus gondola_3` strips: `anim_start.png` frames at different scales (the gondola small at 0 s, large at 0.86 s, small at 2 s); `anim_turn.png` frames the gondola small in a frame the size of its whole orbit with almost no faint context, so it reads as a gondola flying through empty space rather than riding a wheel. The focus pose sheet, by contrast, is framed consistently on the gondola and is the picture that proves it stays level.
- The focus sheet's title bar reads `SCENE → GONDOLA_3 ...` in both the rest and the `--pose quarter` render; nothing on the sheet says the pose. Only `report.md` has `(sheet shows "quarter")`. With two renders side by side I had to rely on the folder name.
- Thin-part advice pulled two ways on `sign_text`: at size 0.15/weight 0.045 the report said `'sign_text' (1.5 cells) ... thicken to two cells`; thickening to 0.06 gave `the counters ... is only 0.0147 wide, 0.489 of the 0.03 cell, and closes up`. Neither message mentions the other constraint; size 0.2/weight 0.05 satisfied both after two tries.
- `check --pose` prints a `posed` line under shared steps (`roof`, `tub`, `seat`) for one placement only (the first gondola's) without saying so, and `posed` lines under the booth's parts that have no joint at all (they are just `move`d by the parent), so "posed" really means "where the output puts it".
- `motor_vent` at 0.02 thick: `warning ... only 0.02 thick, 0.667 of the 0.03 cell ... Thicken it to 0.06 (two cells) or raise the grid (set grid 512)`: clear and correct; I thickened.
- Watertight row on the final ride: 41 edges "in 'tie' alone ... all on the plane z = -0.45" — the mirrored frame's tube face on a sample plane; the advice (nudge by a fraction of a cell) is clear; I left it since renders are unaffected.
- The viewer page (read as text): buttons Perspective/Front/Right/Top, Wireframe, Grid, `▶ turn`, `▶ start`, `▶ All`, `❚❚`, a `loop` checkbox (checked), speed ¼×/½×/1×/2×, and a scrub range input. As documented.

## 4. What worked well enough to keep

- `check`'s sizes plus the joint tree with pivots and axes: the whole rig was verified before any picture, and the `posed` lines told me which gondola is lowest in each pose. `angle("wheel")[0]` on an axis joint reads exactly as the doc says.
- Assert messages with both sides and the pose name (`in pose quarter fails: ... is 0.4 > 0.6`). `at(ride, "name")` in a pose carrying every joint above it, so a per-pose clearance assert is one line.
- The `|`-precedence and painted/unpainted warnings, which caught my two real bugs at once with the line number and the fix.
- `pose()` from a def with parameters, `xform` not needed; `axis=` joints with a single angle; the `full` pose for a seamless loop, exactly as the doc's rotor note says.
- The focus close-up: re-meshed at its own cell, faint context with a caption, its own watertight row, and slices through the gondola showing the tub, seat and posts.
- `set zoom 1.25` and `--beauty-size 768` gave a presentable beauty render in one extra pass; `glow` materials read in it.
- steps.png and the report's step table (with `In output`) made every misplaced part findable.

## 5. Three changes that would have helped most

1. Make bound queries and the printed `anchors` of a nested joint's step world-posed under `--pose`/`pose=` (matching the `posed` line), or state plainly in the assertions and rigs sections that they are not and that `at(outermost, name)` is the per-pose measurement, with an example assert about "the lowest part in pose X".
2. A number-to-string join (`"gondola_" + i` or `str(i)`) so a def can name its joint and its anchor per copy, plus the doc sentence that copies which must move on their own are a def per copy with a named step each, not `place()`.
3. Speed of the loop on a rig: cache or cheapen `pieces()` asserts (or `check --no-asserts` / skip them on `--quick`), and let a render skip or select animation strips (`--no-anims`, `--anim turn`) since the two strips were two thirds of every 3.5-minute render. Smaller but in the same spirit: consistent framing across a focused strip, and the pose name in a focused sheet's title bar.
