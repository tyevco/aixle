## 1. The finished arm

A 0.48 × 1.25 × 0.48 arm (one unit = one metre) on a charcoal round base plate at y = 0, facing +z. Joint tree exactly as planned and as `check` prints it: `base` (about y, pivot (0, 0.05, 0)) > `shoulder` (about x at y 0.30) > `elbow` (about x at y 0.72) > `wrist` (`axis=[0,1,0]`, the forearm's own axis, at y 1.06) > `finger_l`, `finger_r` (slides via `xform(move=)`). Orange painted neck, upper arm, forearm and lugs (`material("#e8721c", rough=0.45)`), charcoal hubs/palm/turntable, steel finger bars, rubber pads on the inner tip faces, an iron barrel and chrome rod for the cylinder, which runs up the +x side from a pin lug on the upper arm to an eye lug on the forearm and is rebuilt from `angle("elbow")` with a small `turnx` def (barrel in the shoulder's frame, rod in the elbow's frame); `eye_check = at(elbow, "eye")` matched my computed eye in every pose (reach [0.1,0.88,0], tucked, wave). Poses: rest, reach (shoulder 60, elbow 30: forearm horizontal, tip at z 0.884), tucked (shoulder −45, elbow 140), grip_open (±0.06 slide), grip_closed (∓0.026), wave (base 60, shoulder −30, elbow 50, wrist 45), plus two combined poses `hold` and `carry` built by `def arm_pose(name, s, e, f) = pose(...)` because the pick animation needed reach-with-closed-fingers. Five asserts, three animations, watertight, one piece, stands, `set roblox 1`. It came out as planned except two departures forced by the tool: the tucked fold had to open from 150° to 140° (the forearm passed through the upper arm at 150, which only the full-size `--pose carry` sheet showed), and the tucked-clearance assert had to be rewritten on an anchor (see friction 1).

## 3. Friction

1. **Per-pose `clearance` on a nested step measures the rest shape.** Line (as first written): `assert clearance(wrist, turntable) > 0.02, "the gripper clears the base when tucked", pose=tucked`. Expected: the doc's own example form (`assert clearance(hand, face) > 0.02, pose=reach`) to measure the wrist where tucked puts it (posed bottom y 0.38, so ~0.23 to the turntable). Happened: passed silently with 0.89, the rest value; my probe `< 0.5, pose=tucked` failed with `is 0.89 < 0.5`, identical to the rest number. The pose is applied to a step only through its own joint, and `wrist` sits under elbow/shoulder/base. No warning. Workaround: `assert at(arm, "tip")[1] > top(turntable) + 0.02, pose=tucked` (an anchor read through the outer step is posed). The doc should say that `clearance`/bounds on a step below other joints are not posed, and ideally `check` should warn when a pose assert's operands did not change from rest.
2. **No way to layer poses.** Lines: `animation("pick", ["rest", "reach", "grip_closed", "tucked"], ...)` with `pose("grip_closed", finger_l=xform(...), finger_r=xform(...))`. Expected: reach, then the fingers close. Happened: strip frames 1.43–1.79 s showed the arm swung back to vertical, because a pose rests every joint it does not name. Workaround: `def arm_pose(name, s, e, f) = pose(name, ...)` and two extra poses `hold`, `carry`. The doc says `pose()` may be called from a def, which saved it, but a `pose("hold", from="reach", finger_l=...)` or a way to name a pose in another pose would have been the natural thing.
3. **The pose sheet cannot show a gripper.** `poses.png` thumbnails (~250 px) make the fingers 3–4 px; grip_open and grip_closed, reach and hold, tucked and carry are pairwise indistinguishable. I judged them from `check --pose` numbers and the focus pose sheet. A pose sheet could note "these poses differ only in finger_l, finger_r" or auto-zoom to the joints a pose moves.
4. **Focus `anim_pick.png` is unreadable.** `--focus finger_l` with `animation("pick", ...)`. Expected: a strip of the finger close up in each frame like the focus pose sheet. Happened: the finger is a few pixels in six of eight frames, huge in frame 2, an axes triad appears in some frames, and no faint context is drawn anywhere; the framing evidently changes per frame. `anim_wave` and `anim_open_close` under focus were fine (with the wave finger changing size between frames).
5. **A sliding finger looks static in a focus strip.** Focus `anim_open_close.png`: the camera tracks the finger, so the finger sits in the same place in all eight frames and only the ghost palm shifts under it. The motion is only inferable, not visible. A fixed frame over the animation (or a trail/rest ghost) would show a slide.
6. **Passing asserts print no numbers on the terminal.** `check` ends with `asserts: 5 pass (at rest, or in the pose each names)`; the measured values (`0.176 > 0.15`, etc.) exist only in `report.json`. To learn how close the closed fingers were I had to read the JSON. Print the detail for passes too, at least under `--pose`.
7. **Self-collision in a pose is only found by a full-size posed render.** `pose("tucked", shoulder=[-30,0,0], elbow=[150,0,0])`: the thumbnail looked fine; `check --pose tucked` had no warning; only `--pose carry` at full size showed the forearm through the upper arm, and the report described it as `24 edges ... 'fore_body', 'upper_body', all on the plane y = 0.547 ... a surface lying exactly on a sample plane, not a thin part`, which pointed me at the grid rather than at the collision. A "step X passes through step Y in pose Z" warning (a negative clearance between two rigid members) would have caught it at `check`.
8. **Posed watertight edges are unfixable advice.** In `--pose tucked` and `--pose wave` the report blamed `'upper_body', 'pin_lug'` at `x = 0.036` / `x = 0.0503` "all on one plane ... move the part by a fraction of a cell". The plane moves with every pose's grid, so the nudge fix cannot hold across poses; the rest render is watertight. The report should say these are pose-dependent and not worth chasing, or skip the sample-plane advice in a posed render.
9. **Contact faults the report caught only after the render**: `eye_lug` (x 0.045..0.105) sat exactly on the forearm's face at x = 0.045 and the rod crossed the elbow cap's end face; the watertight row named the steps and the point and I fixed both by widening the lugs (0.09 wide, buried 0.01–0.02) and moving the pins to x 0.115. This is the doc's "overlap by a cell" rule and my fault, but `check` had "no warnings": a zero-overlap face between two named parts is detectable without meshing.
10. **`posed` lines are detached from their step name.** `check --pose` prints `posed` on its own indented line under the step, so any grep or skim loses which step it belongs to (my own grep did). Repeat the name on the line, e.g. `wrist  posed  ...`.
11. **`joint` for a slide needs a pivot that means nothing.** `finger_l = joint(finger_bar_l + pad_l, "finger_l", -0.0525, wr + 0.08, 0)`: the pivot only matters for rotate/scale, but the doc never says what to give a joint that only slides. Say so (any point on the part).
12. **`pose("rest")` ambiguity.** The reference says "A rest pose is any pose with no angles, or the name 'rest'". I declared `pose("rest")` as the example does; a probe without it also ran clean and listed no rest pose, so the animation accepted "rest" undeclared. One sentence saying whether to declare it (and whether it then appears on poses.png) would settle it.
13. **Focus title size is the frame, not the part.** `ARM → FINGER_L 0.056 × 0.14 × 0.059` while finger_l is 0.037 × 0.12 × 0.04; nothing says which it is.
14. **Roblox row silent on the rig.** The doc says a jointed model exports joints as nodes and animations as clips; the row reports meshes and triangles but not that three clips were written, nor that no `*Attachment` anchor exists (so it is a MeshPart budget, 10000/mesh, which explains 9994).
15. Speed: `check` ~1 s, `--quick` 1.7 s, full render 20–22 s of which poses+strips ~10 s and roblox ~2.7 s, a posed full-size render 6–11 s, beauty 768 px 4.6 s. Fine; no complaint except that the three strips are redrawn on every full render even when only a material changed (`--no-poses` covers that).

## 4. What worked well

- The joint tree in `check` and the report; nesting was right first time and I never had to guess the hierarchy.
- `check --pose NAME` posed lines: I verified every pose numerically before any picture, including the finger gaps and the tip position.
- `angle("elbow")` + a `turnx` def for the cylinder, and `at(elbow, "eye")` agreeing with it in every pose; the cylinder was pin to pin first time and stayed so.
- `pose()` from a def for combined poses.
- `xform(move=)` slides, `axis=` wrist with a single angle, `ease_ends=0` behaving as documented, and the strip bars stating `ONCE/LOOP EASE 1 ENDS 0` and the keyframe times.
- The failing-assert message with both sides and the line, and `pose=name` on asserts.
- The watertight row naming steps and coordinates: it found the two contact faults exactly.
- The focus sheet's faint context with its caption, the focus pose sheet, and `--out` keeping close-ups from overwriting the main render.
- The report.md Animations line and the viewer's per-animation buttons matched the program.

## 5. Three changes that would have helped most

1. Make per-pose asserts actually posed for `clearance` and the bound queries on steps under outer joints (or warn loudly when a `pose=` assert's operands are unchanged from rest), and document the rule precisely.
2. Detect member-through-member collisions in a pose at `check` (negative clearance between sibling rigid parts, e.g. fore_body vs upper_body), and stop the posed watertight row from prescribing grid nudges.
3. Fix the focus animation strips: keep one frame over the whole clip so a slide is visible, draw the faint context in every frame, and never let the framing jump (the `pick` strip); and print passing assert values on the terminal.
