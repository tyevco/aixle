# Round 7 fox: rig, poses, animations and the Bedrock export

Program: `/home/user/aixle/.claude/worktrees/round7-fox/dogfood/fox.aix`. Renders in `out/fox/` (final) and `out/fox-sit/` (the sit pose at full size). 11 renders used (5 full with `--beauty`, 1 full, 1 posed full, 4 quick, three of those quick ones on scratch probe copies outside the worktree). Nothing committed; nothing outside `dogfood/` and `out/` touched. Full render ~12–13 s, quick ~4 s, `check` ~1 s.

## 1. The finished fox

A Bedrock box fox, 0.40 × 0.65 × 1.21 blocks (body + head 0.83 long, the two-segment tail adds the rest; the brief's 0.9 was for the body), every part a whole-sixteenth cube built with a `cube(ox, oy, oz, w, h, d)` helper on the lattice, grown a third of a pixel (upward only in y so the feet stay on y = 0) so touching bones fuse; the voxeliser absorbed the growth and wrote exactly 11 whole-pixel cubes. Bones: `body` (root, pivot on the floor under the hips at (0, 0, -3 px)), `head` (skull 4×4×4, snout 2×2×2 with a black nose pixel, two 1×2×1 ears with black tip pixels, eyes as a decal), `front_left/right`, `back_left/right` (2×3×2, hip pivots, black paw pixel), `tail` (2×2×3) with `tail_tip` (2×3×3, white) nested. Materials: fur orange, white chest (the body's own front bottom pixel row), white tail tip, black paws/ear tips/nose, dark eye decal. Poses: rest, look, walk_a, walk_b, crouch (xform move + scale), pounce (xform rotate + move + scale), sit, breath_l, breath_r. Animations: idle (3 s, loop, ease=1, breath via body scale 1.04 and tail/tail_tip sway), walk (0.6 s loop, linear), pounce (`times=[0, 0.3, 0.8, 0.95, 1.4]`, loop=0, ease=1: crouch held 0.5 s, 0.15 s snap to the leap). Everything in the plan was built; the one departure is that the white chest, paws, ear tips and nose ended up as painted pixel cubes rather than decals, because decals did not reach the Bedrock texture (below). Report: watertight yes, 1 piece, stands, no warnings, assert passes, `Materials: white, fur, black, eye`.

## 2. Rig verification

Poses (from `poses.png`, then `--pose sit` at full size):
- look: head turned ~30° to +x and tilted. Right first time.
- walk_a / walk_b: diagonal pairs forward/back, mirror of each other. Right first time.
- crouch: body flattened and lowered, head down, legs splayed. First version sank the feet 0.06 under the floor (the body's `move` carries the legs, which are inside the root joint); fixed by a smaller move plus splayed legs (now -0.04 in the box, note only).
- pounce: airborne, nose slightly up, front legs reaching, back legs trailing, body stretched. First version had the tail pointing down: I had the sign of the tail's x rotation backwards (for a part pointing to -z, +x lifts it). Fixed.
- sit: body pitched back on its rump, back legs folded forward along the floor, front legs vertical, tail curled on the floor, head level. First version had the tail hanging 0.21 below the floor and the front feet in the air; `check --pose sit`'s posed lines showed both, fixed in two passes (tail -25 / tail_tip 45, body move -1.5 px).
- breath_l / breath_r: tail swung to each side; the 4 % breath is invisible in a thumbnail (expected).

Animations (strips): idle shows the tail swinging left then right with an eased bar; walk shows the legs alternating over 8 frames; pounce shows rest → crouch (held through frames 2–5) → airborne at 1.0 s → landing at 1.25 s → rest, with `EASE 1` and the keyed times in the bar. All right first time once the poses were.

Bedrock files (`model.geo.json`, `model.animation.json`), checked against the docs and the hand-made hatchling rig:
- Geometry: `format_version 1.16.0`, `geometry.fox`, texture 32×32, `visible_bounds`. 8 bones, parents right (`head`, legs, `tail` → `body`; `tail_tip` → `tail`), pivots right with z negated: body [0,0,3], head [0,6,-3], front_left [2,3,-3], tail [0,5,4], tail_tip [0,5,7]. Cubes: body 1 (6×5×8 from origin [-3,2,-4]), head 4, legs 1 each, tail 1, tail_tip 1 = 11, all whole-pixel, sub-pixel growth gone, the adjacent paw/leg and ear/tip cubes merged into one cube each. Same shape as `hatchling.geo.json` (which also has `rotation` on its wing bones; the fox has none, correct). The x coordinates are unchanged from the source (front_left at +1..3), as the doc's "half turn + mirror = flip of z" implies.
- Animation: `format_version 1.8.0`, `animation.fox.idle` (`loop: true`, length 3), `animation.fox.walk` (`loop: true`, 0.6), `animation.fox.pounce` (no loop key, 1.4), like the hatchling's `flap` (no loop) and `glide` (loop). Channels per bone match the poses: idle → body `scale` (1 → 1.04 → 1), tail and tail_tip `rotation` y (±20, ±15); walk → the four legs, 3 keys each, ±30 as posed; pounce → body `rotation` (-12 x), `position` ([0, -0.5, 0] in the crouch, [0, 5, -5] at the leap: pixels, z negated), `scale` ([1.05, 0.8, 1.05] then [0.95, 0.95, 1.15]), head 18 → 10, front legs -30 → -45, back legs 30 → 40, tail -10 → 25, tail_tip 0 → 15. Times: walk 0 / 0.3 / 0.6; eased clips baked to 8 keys per segment (33 keys per channel; 0.0375 s apart in the 0.3 s segment, 0.0625 through the hold). Rotation signs are copied unchanged from the pose; positions are pixels with z negated; scale unchanged. Whether unchanged rotation signs are what Bedrock expects after the z flip I could not tell from the docs (a reflection should negate x and y rotations unless Bedrock's own convention compensates, and the doc only says "in the entity's frame"); the hatchling example round-trips its Bedrock numbers unchanged, which suggests it is intended.
- Texture (`model.geo.png`, read as a texel dump): after the rework every window is right — fur everywhere, the body's north window white on its bottom two rows and the underside's front row, flanks white at the front-bottom corner, paws black on the soles and the legs' bottom row, ear tips black on top, nose black on the snout's top-front pixel, tail tip white. No eye texels (the decal).
- Viewer page: buttons Perspective, Front, Right, Top, Wireframe, Grid, "▶ idle", "▶ walk", "▶ pounce", "▶ All", pause, a `loop` checkbox (checked), a speed select (¼×, ½×, 1×, 2×), a scrub range 0..1; loads three@0.160.0 through an importmap from a CDN and says "this page needs a network connection the first time". 2.5 MB (it embeds the 1.9 MB GLB: 44 k triangles at grid 128 for eleven boxes).

## 3. Friction

- **Decals do not reach the Bedrock face texture on most faces, and the docs do not say how a texel is painted.** Lines (original): `chest_region = box(6.6 * px, 2.6 * px, 3 * px) | move(0, 3.2 * px, 4 * px)`, `body_p = cube(...) | paint(fur) | decal(chest_region, white)`; `eyes = (sphere(0.55 * px) | move(1.3 * px, 6.6 * px, 7.1 * px)) + ...`; `decal(ear_tips, black)`; `decal(box(2.6 * px, 1.4 * px, 2.6 * px) | move(..., 0.5 * px, ...), black)` on each leg. Expected: the face windows painted where the sheet shows the decals. Got (texel dump): only fur, the tail tip's white and the chest on the *underside* window; no eyes, no black paws, no ear tips, no nose, no chest on the front (north) window. Probes: paws appeared only when the region ran 1.5 px *below* the sole (region to -0.2 px: nothing; to -1.5: painted); a region pushed 1 px into the solid changed nothing; eyes with a region reaching from 3 px inside to 3 px in front of the face: nothing; a decal on the back painted the south window and one on the +x flank painted the east window; a whole cube painted black paints all six windows; a painted skin 0.9 px thick on the snout's front painted nothing; a painted slab 0.225 px proud of the body's front painted nothing; the ears' *down* windows came out in the skull's colour. I could not find a rule fitting all of this (not "sample at the voxel centre", not "half a pixel outside the face", not "nearest surface point"). The doc says only "each cube face with its own window in `model.geo.png` painted from the materials". Workaround: everything that must reach the texture is a whole voxel-aligned painted cube (paw pixel, ear tip pixel, nose pixel, chest rows), eyes left as the decal the brief asked for, absent from the texture. Six of eleven renders went here.
- **`model.geo.png` cannot be read as a picture.** 32×32, with the unused area opaque black (0,0,0), so black paws are indistinguishable from filler and the Read tool shows a smear; I had to write a PNG decoder to dump texels as letters. The hand-made hatchling texture convention would be transparent filler.
- **`check` says "no warnings" for a file `render` then warns about.** With the 0.9 px nose skin: `check` → `no warnings`; `render` → `warning: minecraft: 'nose' (line 29) is only 0.056 units thin, 0.9 of the 0.063 cell: it may be missing or broken in the geometry. Thicken it to 0.125 (two cells). (a voxel is 0.063 at 16 pixels per block; set minecraft 32 halves it)`. Good warning, wrong stage: the docs say `check` prints every warning.
- **Tip-over warning in a pose measures the footprint at the lowest point, not the floor.** `render --pose sit`: `warning: The centre of mass (0, 0.251, -0.081) is 0.368 units outside the base's footprint: the model would tip over. Widen the base or move weight over it.` with `Base footprint | 12-sided hull of the points within 0.014 of y = -0.017` — the tail tip's corner. The fox rests on rump, paws and tail at y ≈ 0. Ignored.
- **`check --pose` notes suggest `ground()` for a posed model.** `note: the lowest point of the surface is at y = -0.04; pipe the model through ground() to rest it on y = 0` in `--pose crouch`; grounding would move the rest pose and the export. Also the posed `fox` box is loose (`y -0.28..0.79` in sit) while the note's -0.02 is the true figure; the box alarmed me first.
- **Rotation signs for a rig are in the docs only as the generic rule.** I got the tail wrong first time (`tail=[-20, 0, 0]` in `pounce` lowered it). A line such as "for a limb hanging down +x swings it back; for a tail pointing to -z +x lifts it; for a head +x is nose down" beside the rig section would have saved a pose iteration.
- **Where to put the root pivot for a squash/hop is undocumented.** `pose("crouch", body=xform(move=[0, -1 * px, 0], scale=[1.05, 0.8, 1.05]))` pushed the legs (children of the root) 0.06 under the floor; a pivot on the floor keeps the feet down under `scale`, but any `move` down still sinks them. The hatchling example moves its body down 1 px the same way.
- **The 16-px lattice pattern lives only in the hatchling example**, not the docs: the `cube` helper, the 0.35 px growth so bones fuse, and that the voxeliser rounds the growth away. Copying it worked, but the growth also put the feet at y = -0.01 (`note: the lowest point ... y = -0.01`) until I grew upward only. Worth a paragraph under `set minecraft`.
- **The report's Minecraft row says "x is authored mirrored, as Bedrock draws it"** but the entity file has `front_left` at +x exactly as in the source; for an entity the net effect is a z flip, and the sentence misled me for a while.
- **Entity rotation conventions are not spelled out.** `docs/language.md` says the export "writes the bone rotations in the entity's frame"; nothing says the sign of each axis relative to the pose, nor that positions come out in pixels with z negated (which I only learnt by reading the JSON). I could verify the numbers but not their correctness in game.
- **The animation file is bloated.** `ease=1` bakes 8 keys per segment: 33 keys per channel, 8 identical keys through the 0.5 s crouch hold, tail_tip carrying 17 zero keys before it moves, 2291 lines / 42 KB for three clips; the hatchling's hand-made file uses 3–5 keys per channel. The doc says "at the sampled times" without a number. Bedrock has per-key `lerp_mode`, and a hold is two keys.
- **The pounce strip's bar was cut off at quick size.** `animation("pounce", ..., times=[0, 0.3, 0.8, 0.95, 1.4], loop=0, ease=1)` → the 932 px quick strip's bar ended at `... CROUCH 0.8S → POUNCE 0.9` with the last key missing; the full-size strip (1466 px) shows all five. The eased `idle` strip cannot show a 4 % breath at all (expected, but the strip could print the values).
- **Eyes as a sphere decal look like a ragged smear on the quick sheet** (per-vertex colouring, as documented); box regions of one pixel read better on a box model.
- **The viewer page depends on a CDN** ("needs a network connection the first time"); no offline path.
- Slow-ish: poses 2.4 s + three strips 5.3 s of a 13 s full render, `hierarchy` 0.9 s; fine, and `--no-poses` exists.

## 4. What worked

`joint`/`pose`/`xform`/`animation` syntax read naturally and nesting `tail_tip` inside `tail` just worked; `check --pose NAME` with `posed` lines found every floor collision before a render; `poses.png` and `anim_*.png` were enough to judge every pose and clip, and `--pose sit --out out/fox-sit` for the one that needed a close look; the voxeliser turned grown, overlapping cubes into exactly the 11 whole-pixel cubes I authored, with bones, parents and pivots correct and the paw/leg and ear/tip sub-cubes merged; the animation export picked exactly the channels each pose used and honoured `times`, `loop` and `ease`; the report's Assembly section (joints, poses, animations) and Minecraft row summarised the export accurately; the thin-part warning for the nose was precise; the beauty render made a presentable fox at the default light with no tuning; `--quick` also writes the Bedrock files, which made texture probes 4 s each.

## 5. Three changes that would have helped most

1. Make decals and thin painted skins reach the Bedrock face texture (or document exactly which point paints each face texel, and warn when a material or decal lands on no texel), and give `model.geo.png` a readable companion: transparent filler plus an upscaled, window-labelled preview or a per-bone/face colour table in the report.
2. Document the entity export as a table: pose angle → keyframe sign per axis, `move` → pixels with z negated, `scale` unchanged, bones ← joints with their cubes, and correct the "x is authored mirrored" sentence for entities; add the lattice recipe (`cube` helper, growth, growth rounded away) to the `set minecraft` paragraph.
3. Compact the Bedrock animation (collapse holds to two keys, drop leading constant keys, fewer samples or `lerp_mode`), run the Minecraft thinness warning in `check`, and in a pose measure the footprint at the floor and drop the `ground()` advice.
